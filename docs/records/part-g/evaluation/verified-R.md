# Book R: verified continuity errors

Both audits (R-1: 15 claims; R-2: 13) merged into 18 distinct claims, checked against the text under
the pre-registered rules (see `verified-P.md`). Quotes `quotes.py` could not match were read in the book.

## Accepted (12)

**Minor (7)**
1. **Pell's years in the Nursery** (R-1 #1 serious, R-2 #1 minor). "eleven pictures … one a year since she was taken" and "a hundred and thirty-two times" (Ch 1), against six years: "every night for six years in the crèche … and then never again", "had not touched her daughter in six years" (Ch 13), "once a month for six years" (Ch 19); she is twelve.
2. **Anselm's running balance** (R-1 #2, R-2 #3). In the current volumes his pencilled figure is "a number in the low millions", still falling (Ch 2); the sheet, "the same work … but complete", ends "*Zero. Year 280.*" (Ch 2).
3. **The *Grace* "passes Gallows tomorrow"** (R-1 #9, R-2 #5). Said the night before she leaves (Ch 16); the run takes "four days" (Ch 10), and she is still "eighteen hours behind us" days later (Ch 19).
4. **Corin's "twenty years"** (R-1 #10, R-2 #6). His first circle was the night Tamsin died, "in the three hundred and twenty-sixth year" (Ch 17); it is now the 341st.
5. **"Three nights ago" in the Reliquary** (R-1 #11, R-2 #7). Ch 20; the visit (Ch 11) was at least thirteen days before, by the days counted in Ch 14 and 17.
6. **"Three hundred and forty years"** (R-1 #13, R-2 #9). In narration (Ch 17, 20); the headers, 2431 and 2794, make it 363, and the First Auditor's story (Ch 8) agrees with the larger figure.
7. **The line Quist hears** (R-1 #14, R-2 #8). Ch 20: "*I hope it's you, Pell*"; the recording as Ch 19 gives it has "I hope it's you" at its start and no "Pell".

**Trivial (5)**
8. **Where Anselm's sheet ends** (R-2 #2). It stops at "*Zero. Year 280.*", with "And since?" below (Ch 2); later it has "Three hundred and forty-one lines … from *Founding* to the present" (Ch 17).
9. **The forged volume's age** (R-1 #3, R-2). Covering "the hundred and thirty-eighth to the hundred and forty-second years", it was "a hundred and twenty years old" in the 279th (Ch 5).
10. **Thirty-four people in the hall** (R-1 #7, R-2). "Thirty-two children, a girl of nineteen, an old woman of seventy-four and me" (Ch 9) are 35; Ch 12 says "thirty-four people breathing".
11. **When Rook quit** (R-1 #8, R-2 #4). The tithe was ten years ago, Jory died "two years later", and Rook quit "the next week" (Ch 15): eight years, against "seven years ago" (Ch 6).
12. **"Pin operations" in the oldest volumes** (R-1 #12). The Ninth's volumes begin "in the first decades after the Starving" (Ch 2), yet its oldest charged "*pin operations* … at the very beginning, before the Starving" (Ch 20).

## Rejected (6)

- **"Ten days before"** (R-1 #4). Day 10 to day 19, counted inclusively: explainable.
- **The herald's four days and Wren's three** (R-1 #5). Rests on a reconstruction of hours the text does not pin down: ambiguous.
- **Corley among the fourteen** (R-1 #6). The text does not show him at the vote: ambiguous.
- **Ysolde knows the name Maren** (R-1 #15). She may have it from records not shown: explainable.
- **Eleven children, with or without the baby, and "a dozen"** (R-2). "A dozen" is an estimate, and the first "Eleven" is ambiguous.
- **"Nineteen years of living above" the Drowned Decks** (R-2). She can have moved there at nineteen: explainable.

**Book R: 12 verified (serious 0, minor 7, trivial 5).**
