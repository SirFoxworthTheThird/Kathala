# Book U: verified continuity errors

Both audits (U-1: 11 claims; U-2: 13) merged into 16 distinct claims, checked against the text under
the pre-registered rules (see `verified-P.md`). Quotes `quotes.py` could not match were read in the book.

## Accepted (12)

**Minor (4)**
1. **Twenty-four days or twelve** (U-1, U-2). Sallow and Idris: "in twenty-four days" (Ch 11); the same night Lark: "Day after tomorrow … Ten days before Firstlight they go into the Levy pens" (Ch 11), which is twelve, as the day counts in Ch 14 and 17 agree.
2. **Teague at the strike of '64** (U-1, U-2). "sixty-three years old" in FY 2291 (Ch 1), yet nineteen at the strike of '64 (Ch 10).
3. **The lungs** (U-1, U-2). "There had been four lungs … One had died when I was nine", and Number Two dies now (Ch 9), which leaves two; "We've got one lung, Tally. One. Number Three" (Ch 9), and Ch 15 builds on it.
4. **The grille's seals** (U-1, U-2). Set during the week before Firstlight Eve (Ch 17); "the lead seals he had set himself thirteen nights ago" on Firstlight morning (Ch 20).

**Trivial (8)**
5. **"Seventy years ago"** (U-1). Sallow, "seventy-one" (Ch 2), "skipped to it too, seventy years ago" (Ch 14), at one year old.
6. **Idris's childhood** (U-1, U-2). Sallow's "nineteen years" since Idris was fifteen makes him seventeen years older than Lark, yet he remembers, "as a child", "Lark in his mother's arms, very small" (Ch 17).
7. **The Ring across or round** (U-2). "Thirty-eight kilometers across" (Ch 1); "thirty-eight kilometers round" (Ch 21).
8. **Thirty-eight days to Day 300** (U-1, U-2). From Day 263, which is thirty-seven, as the "twenty-five marks" to Day 288 agree (Ch 10).
9. **"Two days ago"** (U-1, U-2). Dog Number One was driven home on Day 284 (Ch 16); on Day 287 it is "two days ago" (Ch 19).
10. **The boy of three, sung out twice** (U-1). "the baby and the boy of three, two mornings ago", and later "in the corridor that morning, after I had sung out the boy of three" (Ch 9).
11. **The *Clemency*'s day** (U-1, U-2). Sallow's "forty-eight days" (Ch 5), the four days to find Oake and his eleven to the ship put it about the thirty-third day before Firstlight; Severs: "the thirty-seventh day" (Ch 17).
12. **Four hundred and six** (U-2). Of 412 bonded (Ch 4, 10), six go on the *Patience* and four die in the Barn and off the Spindle (Ch 16), yet "There were four hundred and six of them" (Ch 19).

## Rejected (4)

- **The gallery at "nine hundred meters"** (U-1). The tower's height used loosely for points on it, never a stated height for the gallery: ambiguous.
- **A stair "through the whole height"** (U-2). Where it ends is not said: ambiguous.
- **The cord "two days ago"** (U-2). It can have been on her wrist from two days ago until it was cut: explainable.
- **The Keepers have "stopped taking" Sump novices** (U-2). Sallow's generalisation: a character's belief.
