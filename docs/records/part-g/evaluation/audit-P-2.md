# Continuity audit: Book P

Scope: all 21 chapters, read in full. Myths and propaganda the book shows to be wrong (the Purchase, the Blank Share as "the Company's share", the Tether-Woman, "nobody dies on the floor") are not listed. The book sets them up and then exposes them on purpose.

1. **Hesketh dies before he could have made Maren chief**
   - Chapters: 1 vs 7, 16, 19
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 1: "her own chief, Hesketh, who had gone into Gall the year Abel was born". Ch. 4 gives Abel as "twenty-nine years old".
     - Ch. 7: Odile says "You did four years at twenty kilometres. Before Hesketh made you chief." Maren has been chief "for nine years" (Ch. 1).
     - Ch. 16: "TSOLO stencilled in white across the back where it had said HESKETH for twenty years", and "the way Hesketh had taught her twenty-six years ago".
     - Ch. 19: "the way Hesketh had taught her to write an orbit down when she was a runner of twenty".
   - Why it fails: Chapter 1 puts Hesketh's death 29 years back, when Maren was 12. The later chapters have Hesketh alive and teaching her at 15 and at 20, and making her chief 9 years ago.
   - Severity: **minor**

2. **Maren's career is longer than her age allows**
   - Chapters: 1 (and 4, 16)
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 1: "Maren had been tether-chief for nine years and on the line for twenty-six before that".
     - Ch. 1: "[Ekene] was fifty-two, eleven years older than she was", so she is 41. The same age follows from "Maren was fifteen and Abel was three" with Abel at 29.
     - Ch. 16: "the way Hesketh had taught her twenty-six years ago".
   - Why it fails: 9 + 26 = 35 years on the line at age 41 means she started at 6. Chapter 16 implies 26 years in all, starting at 15, and Chapter 1 says recruits come up from the Camp at about sixteen.
   - Severity: **minor**

3. **The Clement's six marines become eight**
   - Chapters: 7 vs 10
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 7: "'How many?' 'Six. Sergeant called Oyelowo.'" Senna also says "They've got six marines".
     - Ch. 10 accounts for: Sergeant Oyelowo; "There was a marine in the cold hold"; "the marine on the *Clement*'s side of it sitting against the bulkhead with his hands on his head"; "One was a Combine marine, a big man, face down" (dead on the bridge); and Hale's "Four marines, eleven hands, the cargo master" in the crew deck.
   - Why it fails: that is at least eight marines, seven even without the sergeant, on a ship carrying six.
   - Severity: **minor**

4. **The Ocampo "household of five" has six people**
   - Chapters: 2, 5 (vs 14, 20)
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 2: "Household of five", and "The floor is three hundred an hour for five". Pilar, the speaker, says: "Mama's at the plant. Papa's at the plant. Abuela's asleep. Tomás and Juli are asleep."
     - Ch. 5, with Abuela still alive: "two hundred litres an hour for five people".
     - Ch. 14, after Abuela's death: "Pilar's mother is there too, and her father, and the two little ones".
   - Why it fails: with the grandmother alive the household is six (Pilar, two parents, Abuela, Tomás, Juli). It only becomes five after her death, which is when Ch. 20 correctly says "Household of five".
   - Severity: **minor**

5. **The air "floor" figures in the Cells contradict the established floor**
   - Chapters: 2, 5, 8 vs 11
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 2: the floor is "three hundred an hour for five".
     - Ch. 5: "Two hundred for five. … That is the new floor."
     - Ch. 8: the prisoners get "Allowance three-twenty an hour", "a little above the old floor".
     - Ch. 11: "I turn it to the floor. Two hundred and twenty." and "Nobody can live on ninety."
   - Why it fails: the new floor is 200, not 220. And five people are expected to live on 200 (40 each), so 90 for one person cannot be unsurvivable.
   - Severity: **minor**

6. **Siege day count does not match the calendar**
   - Chapters: 2, 5, 8
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 2: Lampfast is the "twelfth of June". The fleet arrives that evening and the first slug lands before dawn.
     - Ch. 5: "On the eighteenth day of the siege". Ch. 8 dates that crash: "the thirtieth of June".
     - Ch. 8: "Nine days after South Lock", and "Now, eight days into July and twenty-eight days into the siege".
   - Why it fails: if 30 June is day 18, then 8 July is day 26 and 9 July is day 27. Twenty-eight days would be 10 July.
   - Severity: **trivial**

7. **How far off the Lamp's closest pass is, on 8 August**
   - Chapters: 11, 17, 20
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 11: "The Lamp makes its closest pass to Gall on the ninth of August."
     - Ch. 17: "We go out at the start of the eighth", then "We're three days from its closest pass", and later the same day "two days from its closest pass".
     - Ch. 20, still the 8th: "one day from the Lamp's closest pass". The broadcast is "on the ninth of August, at the moment the Lamp makes its closest pass".
   - Why it fails: on 8 August the pass is one day away. Chapter 17 says three days and then two within the same few hours.
   - Severity: **minor**

8. **Distance from the Wick to the Gull and the ridge**
   - Chapters: 17 vs 20
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 17: "The tug is fifty metres from where Corin set the *Wick* down". The shaft is "ten metres along the ridge's foot from the tug". From the cryostat, "the line runs on across the crust to the *Wick*, thirty metres off".
     - Ch. 20: "in a dipper skiff, under a black ridge, two hundred metres from a tug", and "the place on the open ice where the *Wick* was sitting a minute ago, two hundred metres from the ridge".
   - Why it fails: the Wick has not moved, but the same spot is 30 to 50 m away in one chapter and 200 m in the next.
   - Severity: **minor**

9. **Ama Venn's "nine days" on the Rime**
   - Chapters: 14 vs 20
   - Quotes:
     - Ch. 14: relocation begins "on the thirty-first of July". Ama is still in South Lock that day, and Isaura leaves "in the morning", on 1 August.
     - Ch. 20: "'The sixth,' says Malachy. … 'She'd been mending their filters. … Nine days without a stop.'"
   - Why it fails: from 31 July or 1 August to 6 August is at most six or seven days on the Rime, not nine.
   - Severity: **minor**

10. **"Five weeks" since the Throat that are really three**
    - Chapters: 6, 15 vs 21
    - Quotes:
      - Ch. 6: "They come out of the Corva lane on the twelfth of April".
      - Ch. 15, on 29 April: Szabo says "I've been on the *Hymn* for three weeks."
      - Ch. 21, on 2 May: "For the first time in five weeks, since the Throat, the cockpit isn't lit by it", and the moon "whose shadow they've been watching cross the face of the giant for five weeks".
    - Why it fails: 12 April to 2 May is about three weeks. Even counting from Cinderhaven it is barely more.
    - Severity: **minor**

11. **Abel's drilling hours do not add up**
    - Chapter: 16
    - Quotes: "He landed at the ninth hour"; "He drilled. It took him six hours."; "At the thirteenth the auger stopped."
    - Why it fails: 9 + 6 = 15, not 13.
    - Severity: **trivial**

12. **The "second T" in a name with one T**
    - Chapters: 13 vs 19
    - Quotes:
      - Ch. 13: "MAREN TSOLO, at the top of the first column, in shaky capitals, pressing so hard the awl skidded on the second T".
      - Ch. 19: "with the long scratch where the awl had skidded on the T".
    - Why it fails: "MAREN TSOLO" contains only one T.
    - Severity: **trivial**

13. **"Two words" that are four**
    - Chapter: 20 (vs 19)
    - Quote: "Above the line, two words. *WE SENT IT ROUND*". Chapter 19 shows Maren cutting the same line, "*WE SENT IT ROUND*".
    - Why it fails: the line is four words.
    - Severity: **trivial**

14. **The egg's stencil: three lines or four**
    - Chapters: 7, 17, 21 vs 20
    - Quotes:
      - Ch. 7, Ch. 17 and Ch. 21 all show three lines: "SANITATION AGENT / PATTERN H / FACTOR'S AUTHORITY ONLY". Ch. 17 is Isaura's own look at it: "in neat old capitals, three lines."
      - Ch. 20, Isaura's broadcast: "It's a grey egg. It's got four lines stencilled on it and a green wheel."
    - Why it fails: Isaura has seen the three lines herself, a day earlier. Mattias's "four lines" in Ch. 11 belonged to the recognition card, not to the egg.
    - Severity: **trivial**

15. **Who said "cut the line to save the ship" in the Council**
    - Chapters: 11 vs 17
    - Quotes:
      - Ch. 11: the fur collar belongs to "an old woman in a fur collar near the front". The proverb comes from "somebody on the back bench", with "a few others murmur it after him".
      - Ch. 17: "a man in a fur collar on a back bench in the old bridge saying *cut the line to save the ship*".
    - Why it fails: Isaura merges two different people and gets the sex of one and the seat of the other wrong in her recollection.
    - Severity: **trivial**

16. **Where three-year-old Abel was the night their mother died**
    - Chapters: 4 vs 13 (and 16)
    - Quotes:
      - Ch. 4: "her brother, whom she had carried on her hip through the third shaft when their mother went into the cloud". The third shaft is a Camp warren on Kettle.
      - Ch. 13: "he'd gone there [the Belly] the night their mother went into Gall, and she had crawled along the cable tray … and found him … three years old".
      - Ch. 16: "lowered him into his bunk when he was three and asleep in the Belly".
    - Why it fails: on that night he is placed in the Camp on Kettle in one chapter and aboard the orbiting *Patience* in another.
    - Severity: **trivial**

17. **"Nobody unshared" has left Kettle, yet Dasha has**
    - Chapters: 14 vs 5, 17
    - Quotes:
      - Ch. 14: "I've never been off Kettle; nobody unshared has."
      - Ch. 5: Dasha, an unshared rigger, says "Twelve years I've lived on the Ninth North". Ch. 2 states "Nobody shared lives on the Ninth".
      - Ch. 17: Dasha has "done this on the giant's moons, she says, on the ice-rafts that the dipper fleet sometimes chases for water".
    - Why it fails: an unshared person has demonstrably been off Kettle, which the narrator's flat statement denies.
    - Severity: **trivial**

**Counts by severity: serious 0, minor 10, trivial 7 (total 17).**
