# Book T: verified continuity errors

Both audits (T-1: 18 claims; T-2: 19) merged into 25 distinct claims, checked against the text under
the pre-registered rules (see `verified-P.md`). Quotes `quotes.py` could not match were read in the book.

## Accepted (11)

**Minor (5)**
1. **Three days go missing** (T-1 serious, T-2 minor). "at nine in the morning, six days before the Burning" (Ch 11), then "The next day was the day before the day before the Burning" (Ch 14).
2. **Benn's age when the voyage opened** (T-2 serious, T-1 minor). "He was seventy-one" (Ch 1); "He'd been sixty, opening this voyage" (Ch 4), against a voyage about two years old ("an entry from two years ago", Ch 10).
3. **Gallows Reach's depth** (T-1, T-2). "a glacier eight hundred metres thick"; the Haskells live "Eleven hundred metres down" (Ch 2).
4. **Ottilie's age** (T-1, T-2). "She'd been fifty-four then" when Hollis was twelve, twenty-nine years ago (Ch 2); Marsh: "She was sixty-three" (Ch 5).
5. **Calla and Jory** (T-1, T-2). She did "four years down there" and came back "in the six years since" (Ch 5); Jory was struck "Seven years ago", yet she was at home and quarrelling with him that week (Ch 14).

**Trivial (6)**
6. **"The other seventeen" Ordinances** (T-1, T-2). Of "twenty-two articles" (Ch 1), after six are recited.
7. **The brass instruments** (T-1, T-2). "bought … at twenty and kept on my shelf for nineteen years" (Ch 8), when Hollis is 41.
8. **The Widow's weld** (T-1, T-2). "a bead of slag three centuries old" (Ch 3), though the divers "cut the weld on the Widow last spring" and "Gorse welded" it again.
9. **Rask's round** (T-1, T-2). "I came back on the twelve o'clock round. Half past eleven, it was … I'm slow these days" (Ch 14).
10. **Three hundred in the Wake** (T-2). "There were three hundred of them in the Wake" (Ch 4, again Ch 10 and 13), against "two hundred and forty awake" (Ch 1) and a rotation of "two hundred and thirty" (Ch 10).
11. **The ring that is gone** (T-2). "The stump of the ring is gone entirely", and a few lines on, "Down the ring to the forward spoke" (Ch 18).

## Rejected (14)

- **Fourteen months in the dark** (T-1, T-2). Can be counted from the last port rather than from boarding at Callis: explainable.
- **Hollis's strike count, 308 or 309** (T-1). Whether Ch 2's "three hundred and eight" already counts the Haskells is not said: ambiguous.
- **The verifier "due tomorrow"** (T-1, T-2). "I thought he was due tomorrow" is a character's mistaken belief.
- **Gorse "not inside a shell in twenty years"** (T-1). Welding the hatch need not take him inside: explainable.
- **Coil cooling** (T-1). Bleeding heat for weeks and cooling six hours before a jump are different things: explainable.
- **The one-metre plate over a two-metre shaft** (T-1, T-2). The "two metres across" is the Hall's disc, not the recovery port: no clash shown.
- **The Warden who "signed them forty years ago"** (T-1). "your Warden" may be the office, as the second auditor also judged: ambiguous.
- **Bastian's "nine days"** (T-1, T-2). "as if it had been waiting behind his teeth for nine days": a figure of speech.
- **Marsh's belt, waist or arm** (T-1). Four centuries apart; a body tethered by a loop can shift: explainable.
- **What Hollis could know** (T-2). He may have read more than the entry shown: ambiguous.
- **The cipher examples** (T-2). Depends on the recited Catechism being complete, as the auditor noted: ambiguous.
- **"Three bars up" and "We had four"** (T-2). Ambiguous, as the first auditor judged.
- **The 318th Burning** (T-2). An anniversary can be counted either way: ambiguous.
- **Mags's "Tuesday"** (T-1, with the verifier). A character's recollection.
