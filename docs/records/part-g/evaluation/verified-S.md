# Book S: verified continuity errors

Both audits (S-1: 16 claims; S-2: 19) merged into 22 distinct claims, checked against the text under
the pre-registered rules (see `verified-P.md`). Quotes `quotes.py` could not match were read in the book.

## Accepted (12)

**Minor (3)**
1. **Nine pilots** (S-1 #1 serious, S-2 minor). "there were nine pilots standing in front of them in a line" and "Nine. Eight debts, and Nico." (Ch 19), but the line is Haldane, the two Mosses, Ida, Jory, Sven, Marguerite, "Corrigan's empty place, which nobody stood in, and then Teo": eight, and Salome names eight.
2. **Mor's years as High Warden** (S-1, S-2). "He had declined every one for nineteen years" (Ch 5), "since he was invested"; "he was High Warden for twenty-five years" (Ch 17).
3. **A Keeper's skiff that "nobody alive had seen … flown"** (S-1, S-2). Ch 3, from Hanne; but "Nine skiffs at the Fifty-Eighth. Seven at the Fifty-Ninth" (Ch 6), eleven and twenty-two years ago, and Hanne herself says Pike "flew escort for her, at the Fifty-Ninth".

**Trivial (9)**
4. **The sleeve names' dates** (S-1, S-2). The Twenty-Sixth and Twenty-Seventh names are explained by acts of "2573" and "2584" (Ch 8); by the book's 4,000-day cycle back from the Thirtieth in 2608, those Draws fell about 2564 and 2575.
5. **"Six weeks parked at the Anchorhold"** (S-1, S-2). Ch 5, for a posting "from the twentieth of November until the Narrowing" on 5 December.
6. **The penitents' cells** (S-2). "Bottom of the spindle, Needle-ward end" (Ch 17); the Needle-ward end is the top: "at the top of the station's spindle, the Needle-ward end" (Ch 5).
7. **"The Testament"** (S-2). Ch 20; the book is "the Inheritance" (Ch 17).
8. **Shaft Nine's three shifts** (S-1). "three shifts' worth, a hundred and forty cutters and loaders" (Ch 7); "all three shifts, two hundred people" (Ch 10).
9. **The fork struck "at the moment the Keepers went into the mouth"** (S-1, S-2). At four in the morning on 1 May (Ch 20), when at four the pilots were standing on Dock Four (Ch 19).
10. **Adaeze's petitions** (S-2). "Two hundred and six of them, at the last three Draws. I've written every one" (Ch 5); the Twenty-Ninth, eleven years ago, was "her first Keeping as archivist" (Ch 8).
11. **When the swell log began** (S-1, S-2). The notebook column "had begun on the night of the first of March" (Ch 13); Ch 1 has its first entry on the second.
12. **The order of the glass layers** (S-1, S-2). First described as thick at the top, then "a long run of medium bands", then "below those, nearer the bottom", the thin irregular ones (Ch 15); the Book of Keepings then puts the thin ones (Fifty-Seventh to Fifty-Fifth) directly under the two thick ones.

## Rejected (10)

- **The long tide's turn** (S-2, serious). The sum put the turn "in the last days of April"; on 11 April the swell "slowed … exactly where the sum … had said it would" (Ch 13). Slowing comes before a turn: ambiguous.
- **The Thirtieth Keeping's clock** (S-1, S-2). The Keeping may have flown in stages between the 5th and the 6th: ambiguous.
- **Nine skiffs, seven accounted for** (S-1, S-2). "a fourth light turned … Then a fifth. Then a sixth" can be read more than one way, as the auditor said: ambiguous.
- **The Lantern House** (S-1). She can have sung her son off from its steps before she preached there: explainable.
- **Juno "nineteen" at her commissioning** (S-1, S-2). Adaeze's recollection: a character's memory.
- **"A month in forty minutes"** (S-1, S-2). At a thousand times, forty minutes is twenty-eight days: no clash.
- **The Tally's last lines** (S-1). Where the second words sit is ambiguous.
- **Band thickness against the skiff counts** (S-2). The thin first band may be the point the chapter is making: ambiguous.
- **The *Ardour* in two places** (S-2). The *Ardour* is not named in Ch 20.
- **"Eighteen days" to the trough** (S-1, S-2). The auditors disagree on which days it was said; spoken estimates: ambiguous.
