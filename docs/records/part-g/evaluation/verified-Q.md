# Book Q: verified continuity errors

Both audits (Q-1: 20 claims; Q-2: 17) merged into 22 distinct claims, checked against the text under
the pre-registered rules (see `verified-P.md` for the wording). Quotes `quotes.py` could not match
were read in the book.

## Accepted (13)

**Serious (1)**
1. **Who built the Tally** (Q-1 #1, Q-2 serious). Basu, "fifty or so" (Ch 4), says "I built it so. I was twenty-six" (Ch 4) and "I've thought about that every night for twenty-six years" (Ch 19). The Tally "has kept you safe for ninety years" (Ch 1) and "was built, ninety years ago" (Ch 4). Nothing says it was rebuilt, and its builder's back door is the plot.

**Minor (4)**
2. **Fen's "three hundred years"** (Q-1 #2, Q-2). Fen: the names were cut "by hand, for three hundred years" (Ch 6). The bell's carrier crossed in 2296 (Ch 3, Fen herself), and the bell left at the Closing in 2387: "Ninety-one years later" (Ch 3).
3. **Marisol Tey's daughter** (Q-1 #3, Q-2). Her card: "b. 3086" (Ch 12); she says "She's six" (Ch 15), in 3089.
4. **Four cassettes** (Q-1 #8, Q-2). Ninian: each burns "a third of her length for three years, or the whole of her for one" (Ch 14); Tamsin, at once: "Four's a year at full length. Barely three at a third."
5. **The Cold Wards** (Q-1 #10). "nobody had lived in the Cold Wards for longer than anybody had been alive" (Ch 2), against Ninian's "I've shortened her six times since then … and another gallery go cold" in his forty years (Ch 14).

**Trivial (8)**
6. **"Five words"** (Q-1 #12). Ch 8: "there were five words", then SHE SAID SHE WOULD COME BACK: six.
7. **Ode's years** (Q-1 #13, Q-2). "thirty years of running throats" (Ch 3); "thirty-four years" (Ch 12, 18).
8. **Kell's bailiffs** (Q-1 #14, Q-2). "Ten men and women" (Ch 3); "there are twelve of them" (Ch 18).
9. **Dacey's four days** (Q-1 #15, Q-2). "the third day out from the Throat" and "listening to the silence of the Sabra system for four days" (Ch 9).
10. **"Two days ago"** (Q-1 #16, Q-2). The bell went into the tank-frame at 2 a.m. on day 48 (Ch 16); at 4 a.m. on day 49 it was "two days ago" (Ch 19).
11. **The night of the break-in** (Q-1 #17, Q-2). "at two in the morning on the twenty-third day" (Ch 17), against the third list on the twenty-fourth and "three nights before" the twenty-seventh (Ch 14, 20).
12. **The keel-ways' age** (Q-1 #18). "three and a half centuries ago" (Ch 20), in narration; the colony opened in 2296 and the Owing is 311 years after 2387, about 400 years.
13. **The one unwatched place** (Q-2). Basu, who "designed the camera net": "There's only one place on Lantern the Tally doesn't watch. The Wick" (Ch 4). Narration: the chapel "was the only room on Lantern with no Company camera in it" (Ch 7).

## Rejected (9)

- **Joss "a hundred metres along" at the middle collimator** (Q-1 #4, Q-2). The collimators' spacing is never given: ambiguous.
- **When the closing phase began** (Q-1 #5, Q-2). The announcement's "Three weeks ago" is the Company's lie, which Basu's account exposes: in-world falsehood.
- **What a child costs** (Q-1 #6, Q-2). A child's own account against a family's cost: explainable in one line, as the auditor noted.
- **Dagny's 611 credits and "by eleven"** (Q-1 #7, Q-2). Which line the eleven is measured from is not said: ambiguous.
- **"Nearer five" thousand Called** (Q-1 #9, Q-2). A character's estimate.
- **The spine winches** (Q-1 #11). Ninian's "sled, on rails, with a winch" need not be "the cargo winches on the spine": explainable.
- **"Long weeks" in the infirmary** (Q-1 #19). Not a count.
- **The approach timetable** (Q-1 #20, Q-2). Turns on whether two clocks agree, which the book does not settle: ambiguous.
- **Gallery Twenty-Two "the last gallery before the Lip"** (Q-2). Galleries 23 and 24 may lie round the rock rather than beyond it: ambiguous.

**Book Q: 13 verified (serious 1, minor 4, trivial 8).**
