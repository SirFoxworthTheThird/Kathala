# Continuity audit: book-U

1. **Firstlight is 24 days away, and also 12 days away, on the same night**
   - Chapters: 11 (also against 5, 8, 14, 17)
   - Quotes: Sallow: "in twenty-four days it will all be over"; Idris, later that night: "In twenty-four days they're going to throw the Drum away". Then Lark, the same night: "Benno's going to the pens," she said. "Day after tomorrow. That's when they take the drawn. Ten days before Firstlight they go into the Levy pens".
   - Why it can't be true: going by Lark, Firstlight is 12 days off, not 24. The later day counts agree with Lark. Idris thinks "for six days" before he opens the grille (ch. 14). Then Firstlight Eve comes "six days" after the grille night (ch. 17). So 24 is the odd one out. It doesn't fit the ch. 5 count either. Sallow gave "forty-eight days" (ch. 5); the *Clemency* came about day 15. Add the eleven days of reading in ch. 11, and the gap to Firstlight comes out well under 24.
   - Severity: **minor**

2. **The grille was resealed six days before Firstlight Eve, but "thirteen nights" before Firstlight**
   - Chapters: 14, 17, 20
   - Quotes: Ch. 17, on Firstlight Eve: "You've had a face on you for six days. Since you went up the gallery that night." Ch. 20, the next morning: "He cut the lead seals he had set himself thirteen nights ago, eight of them".
   - Why it can't be true: Idris resealed the grille on the gallery night (ch. 14). If that was six days before Firstlight Eve, it was about seven nights before Firstlight morning, not thirteen.
   - Severity: **minor**

3. **Hold Fourteen's lungs: four, minus two dead, leaves "one"**
   - Chapters: 9 (and 15)
   - Quotes: "There had been four lungs in Hold Fourteen when I was born. One had died when I was nine. Number Two was the big one…" (Number Two then dies). Veska: "We've got one lung, Tally. One. Number Three." Ch. 15 builds on this: "Number Three died four days after… The whole Hold's breathing the black."
   - Why it can't be true: four lungs minus the one that died when she was nine, minus Number Two, leaves two working lungs, not one. Ch. 15's claim that the whole Hold breathes the black once Number Three dies depends on the miscount.
   - Severity: **minor**

4. **Teague's age at the strike of '64**
   - Chapters: 1, 4, 10 (also 19)
   - Quotes: Ch. 1 (FY 2291): "the gang's tether man, sixty-three years old". Ch. 4: "the strike of '64 alongside Teague". Ch. 10, Teague: "We did it on the Ostrava docks in '64… I was one of the ones they didn't hang. I was nineteen." Ch. 19 repeats it: "Teague at nineteen walking under them".
   - Why it can't be true: in 2291 Teague is 63. In 2264 he would have been 36, not 19. If he was 19, the strike was around 2247, not '64.
   - Severity: **minor**

5. **Idris's age against his childhood memory of baby Lark**
   - Chapters: 2, 17
   - Quotes: Ch. 2: Lark "was seventeen"; Idris first saw the Knot "at fifteen, a novice". Ch. 17, Sallow: "You were fifteen. … I've been sorry for it for nineteen years." That makes Idris 34. Also ch. 17: "Idris had loved it as a child. He had stood on a chair… with his father's hand on his shoulder, and Lark in his mother's arms, very small".
   - Why it can't be true: if Idris is 34 and Lark is 17, Idris was about 17 when Lark was a baby, and already a novice Keeper. He couldn't have watched the lamp lit "as a child" on a chair while Lark was a babe in arms.
   - Severity: **minor**

6. **When the boy of three was sung out**
   - Chapter: 9
   - Quotes: "Two of them died that night. A baby, and a boy of three. I sang them both out in the morning" (the 12th day). On the 14th day: "I had said goodbye to the Hold already, in the corridor that morning, after I had sung out the boy of three". Later that same day: "the baby and the boy of three, two mornings ago."
   - Why it can't be true: the boy was sung out on the morning of the 12th, "two mornings" before. He can't also have been sung out on the morning of the 14th.
   - Severity: **minor**

7. **The *Clemency* came through on the "thirty-seventh day before Firstlight"**
   - Chapters: 5, 8, 17
   - Quotes: Ch. 5, Sallow: "You have forty-eight days." Then: "It took him four days to find the right one." Oake: "A ship is coming through the Eye in eleven days." Ch. 17, Severs: "You falsified an entry in the Transit Register on the thirty-seventh day before Firstlight. The *Clemency*."
   - Why it can't be true: four days plus eleven puts the *Clemency* about 15 days into the 48, so about 33 days before Firstlight. That fits the "thirteen days" between Rook visits in ch. 8. For 37 to be right, Oake would have had to name the ship on the day Sallow spoke, not four days later.
   - Severity: **trivial**

8. **Sol's count of days left on Day 263**
   - Chapter: 10 (against 1 and 4)
   - Quotes: "There were two hundred people in the ballast hold on the night of Day Two Hundred and Sixty-Three." Then: "Thirty-eight. Thirty-eight days, still, to Day Three Hundred." And: "the place in the rows where Day Two Eighty-Eight would be—twenty-five marks along from the last one she had crossed".
   - Why it can't be true: from Day 263 to Day 300 is 37 days, not 38. The earlier counts use the same rule and come out right: 61 left on Day 239 and 53 on Day 247, both counted to Day 300. "Twenty-five marks along" to Day 288 also means Day 263 is already crossed, which leaves 37.
   - Severity: **trivial**

9. **The dog was driven home "two days ago"**
   - Chapters: 16, 19
   - Quotes: Ch. 16 is the night of Day 284 ("since the twelfth dog came out yesterday", the dogs having been pulled on Day 283; "Your clock fires in eighty-one hours"). Ch. 19, after the *Assessor* boards "at the start of second shift on Day Two Hundred and Eighty-Seven": "Number One's dog stood out of its cuff a hand's width, where they had driven it home two days ago".
   - Why it can't be true: the dog went in on Day 284 and Sol comes back late on Day 287. That is three days, not two.
   - Severity: **trivial**

10. **Sallow "skipped to it… seventy years ago" at age 71**
    - Chapters: 2, 14
    - Quotes: Ch. 2: "He was seventy-one". Ch. 14: "But I skipped to it too, seventy years ago, on the Eleventh stair."
    - Why it can't be true: seventy years ago Sallow was about one year old, too young to be skipping rope.
    - Severity: **trivial**

11. **The Spindle's height and the height of the gallery and Barn**
    - Chapters: 2, 4, 16, 20
    - Quotes: Ch. 2: "The Spindle rose nine hundred meters off the rim… and the gallery was a band of glass three-quarters of the way up". Ch. 4: the Barn is "three-quarters of the way up". Ch. 20: the procession goes "from the chapter-house to the gallery, nine hundred meters of stair"; Idris later goes down "for nine hundred meters". Ch. 16: a Proctor drifts out of the Barn's hatch, "nine hundred meters over the Ring".
    - Why it can't be true: three-quarters of 900 m is about 675 m. Ch. 21 itself says the stair runs "through the whole height of the tower, nine hundred meters" and puts the gallery three-quarters of the way up.
    - Severity: **trivial**

Counts: 0 serious, 6 minor, 5 trivial (11 total).
