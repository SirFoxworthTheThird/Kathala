# Continuity audit: book-T

I read all 21 chapters. Each finding below was checked twice against the text. Characters' beliefs that the book shows to be wrong (the Catechism, the Fen songs, Yara taking Marsh's body for Fen's, Quell's "nobody's been into the vault") are not listed.

1. **Benn names one fewer Ordinance than she recited**
   - Chapters: 1
   - Quotes: Annick recites Articles One, Four, Nine, Eleven, Fourteen and Twenty, which is six of "twenty-two articles". Benn then says: "You don't need the other seventeen tonight."
   - Why they conflict: 22 − 6 = 16, not 17.
   - Severity: trivial

2. **Benn's age when Voyage V opened**
   - Chapters: 1, 4, 10 (with 19)
   - Quotes: Ch 1: "He was seventy-one". Ch 4, hearing the voyage's opening entry: "He'd been sixty, opening this voyage." Ch 10: Marit's boarding entry at Callis is "an entry from two years ago". Ch 10 gives Abena's age at boarding as "Age seven" and Ch 19 gives it as "Nine years old".
   - Why they conflict: If the voyage opened about two years ago, Benn was about 69 then, not 60. A Benn of 60 needs an eleven-year voyage, which nothing else allows (see also item 3).
   - Severity: minor

3. **How long the voyage has lasted**
   - Chapters: 10, 19
   - Quotes: Ch 10: "an entry from two years ago … *Osei, Marit.* … Boarded at Callis." Ch 19, Annick on the bonded: "They've been in the dark for fourteen months".
   - Why they conflict: The hold boarded at Callis. It cannot have been aboard both two years and fourteen months. Abena's age going from 7 to 9 supports two years.
   - Severity: minor

4. **The Gallows Reach glacier is shallower than the town dug into it**
   - Chapters: 2 (with 19)
   - Quotes: "they dug the first galleries with hand tools into the face of a glacier eight hundred metres thick … The town goes down into the ice like a set of stairs." Then: "The Haskells lived near the bottom. Eleven hundred metres down".
   - Why they conflict: A gallery 1,100 m down cannot sit inside an 800 m glacier. Ch 19 confirms that under the ice there is only sea ("Eight hundred metres of it at the thinnest, then a sea").
   - Severity: minor

5. **Ottilie Brask's age**
   - Chapters: 2, 5
   - Quotes: Ch 2: Hollis has known her "for twenty-nine years", and when he was twelve "She'd been fifty-four then". Ch 5, Marsh: "She was sixty-three."
   - Why they conflict: 54 + 29 = 83, not 63. (Marsh's "came up through the Office together, forty years ago" fits 63, so the "fifty-four" is the outlier.)
   - Severity: minor

6. **Hollis's strike count**
   - Chapters: 2, 5 (with 8, 20)
   - Quotes: Ch 2 is narrated after the Haskell strike, and that same evening he says "I remember three hundred and eight strikes". The retrospective opening also says "I've struck three hundred and eight people". Ch 5: "'Three hundred and nine,' I said. 'There was one this week.' / 'Haskell.'" Ch 20: "I struck three hundred and nine people … Ewan Haskell was the last."
   - Why they conflict: Calla's exchange makes 308 the total before Haskell, so the total after him is 309. Ch 2 already gives 308 after him.
   - Severity: minor

7. **The verifier's arrival, and Mags's "Tuesday", are a day off the calendar**
   - Chapters: 2, 5, 8
   - Quotes: Ch 2 is twelve days before the Burning. There Marsh says: "The Concordance verifier arrives in two days". Ch 5 begins "ten days before the Burning". That day Hollis says "I thought he was due tomorrow", and the ship docks that day. Ch 8, Mags: "Tuesday was the day after Ottilie died. The day before I'd gone into the dead accounts."
   - Why they conflict: Two days after day −12 is day −10, the day he arrives, so he was due that day, not "tomorrow". Ottilie died the night before day −12, so the day after her death is day −12. Hollis entered the dead accounts at 3 a.m. on day −10, so the day before was day −11. Calla's "three nights ago" and Ch 8's countdown both confirm Ch 5 is day −10.
   - Severity: minor

8. **The Widow's weld: centuries old, yet re-welded last spring**
   - Chapters: 3
   - Quotes: "dogged shut and welded at the rim with a bead of slag three centuries old". Two paragraphs later, Bram and Ole "cut the weld on the Widow last spring … Gorse welded the hatch shut again himself".
   - Why they conflict: The weld was cut and replaced a year ago, so it cannot be three centuries old. The tank was in any case closed in CR 718 (Ch 11), about six centuries earlier.
   - Severity: minor

9. **Gorse hasn't worn a shell in twenty years, but welded the Widow himself**
   - Chapters: 3
   - Quotes: "Gorse welded the hatch shut again himself" (last spring). On the next page: "He is a vast soft man who has not been inside a shell in twenty years".
   - Why they conflict: The Widow's hatch is deep in the flooded spine of the Hulk, so welding it means diving in a shell.
   - Severity: minor

10. **Calla was in the Deep Ice when Jory was struck, yet was at home that week**
    - Chapters: 5, 14
    - Quotes: Ch 5: "She'd done four years down there, for her father's portion. She'd come back up and organised the Hold in the six years since." Jory was struck "Seven years ago. Two years for six hundred marks." Ch 14: Jory borrowed her boots "the week before … I didn't speak to him for three days. Then you came."
    - Why they conflict: By Ch 5's figures Calla was in the penal mines from about ten to six years ago, which covers the strike seven years ago. She could not have been on the surface quarrelling with Jory that week.
    - Severity: minor

11. **The coil takes weeks to cool, except at the Fork**
    - Chapters: 1, 4, 7, 13, 16
    - Quotes: Ch 1: after a jump the coil "bled heat for weeks", and the ship waits at Pell "while the coil cooled". Ch 4: "The Fork is thirty-five days out, at the present rate of cooling". Ch 7: "seventeen days, not twenty-one". Ch 13: "At the Fork, when we come out of fold, the coil has to cool for six hours before it can jump again". Ch 16: "Fold complete. Coil cooling. Six hours to next jump." In the same chapter the Absalom branch is "two jumps; three weeks".
    - Why they conflict: The book sets the cooling time after a jump at weeks, and the whole Pell section depends on it. At the Fork the same cooling takes six hours, with no explanation, and the Fork plot depends on that.
    - Severity: minor

12. **"Eleven hours" of freedom, then three days disappear**
    - Chapters: 11, 14 (with 8, 17, 20)
    - Quotes: Ch 11: the vault reading is "at nine in the morning, six days before the Burning", and Hollis flees that day. Ch 14: "I'd been a free man for about eleven hours". That night Rask visits, then: "The next day was the day before the day before the Burning". Ch 20 counts back from the Burning: "I'd floated past it again six days ago with Thorne".
    - Why they conflict: The day after day −6 is day −5, not day −2. Wren's birthday (day −1, "Sixteen … Tomorrow") and the six-day gap in Ch 20 both fix the dates, so three days between Chapters 11 and 14 are missing.
    - Severity: serious

13. **Hollis's age, from the brass instruments**
    - Chapters: 8 (with 2, 5)
    - Quotes: "Charter-era brass instruments I'd bought in a Saltmarch junk shop at twenty and kept on my shelf for nineteen years".
    - Why they conflict: 20 + 19 = 39. Hollis is 41 everywhere else: lifted at twelve and known to Marsh "twenty-nine years", and in the vault at thirteen, "twenty-eight years" before.
    - Severity: trivial

14. **The Warden who signed the Accords forty years ago has served twenty-two**
    - Chapters: 3, 9
    - Quotes: Ch 3: Corin Asch "has been Warden of Tamar for twenty-two years". Ch 9, Kade, speaking "before your Warden": "since your Warden signed them forty years ago, when the Lane reopened".
    - Why they conflict: Asch took office eighteen years after the signing, so he cannot have signed. This holds unless "your Warden" means the office rather than the man, which the phrasing doesn't suggest.
    - Severity: trivial

15. **Rask's "slow" round arrives early**
    - Chapters: 14
    - Quotes: "I do the rounds at ten and twelve and two." On the ten o'clock round he sees Bastian "at about ten past". Then: "I came back on the twelve o'clock round. Half past eleven, it was, when I got to the grille. I'm slow these days."
    - Why they conflict: Being slow would bring him to the grille after midnight, not half an hour before the round begins.
    - Severity: trivial

16. **Bastian's "nine days"**
    - Chapters: 17 (with 2, 5, 8)
    - Quotes: "as if it had been waiting behind his teeth for nine days."
    - Why they conflict: Ottilie died the night before day −12, and Bastian confesses on Burning Day. By the book's own countdowns that is thirteen days, not nine.
    - Severity: trivial

17. **Marsh's belt: round his waist, later round one arm**
    - Chapters: 18, 20 (with 21)
    - Quotes: Ch 20: "He'd tied himself to the pedestal of the old cradle with his belt — looped it through a handhold on the iron and round his waist". Ch 18, the same body: "tethered by one arm to the iron pedestal of the cradle with a belt".
    - Why they conflict: A belt buckled round the waist cannot end up tethering one arm.
    - Severity: trivial

18. **A one-metre plate covers a two-metre shaft**
    - Chapters: 19, 20, 21
    - Quotes: The recovery port is "a round iron plate a metre across" (Ch 19), "a round plate a metre across" (Ch 20 and Ch 21). Beneath it: "A shaft. Two metres across" (Ch 20 and Ch 21). In Ch 21 the plate "falls through its own frame" down that shaft.
    - Why they conflict: A one-metre lid cannot close, or jam across the mouth of, a shaft two metres wide.
    - Severity: minor

Counts by severity: serious 1, minor 11, trivial 6 (total 18).
