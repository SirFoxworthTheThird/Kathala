# Continuity audit: book R

1. **How long Pell has been in the Nursery**
   - Chapters: 1, 4, 13, 19
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 1: "eleven pictures that Pell had drawn and sent down on the visitation shuttle, one a year since she was taken"
     - Ch 1: "Maren had been up here a hundred and thirty-two times"
     - Ch 4: "under Pell's eleven drawings"
     - Against these, Ch 1: "since the first visitation, when Pell was six" and "I'm twelve"
     - Ch 4: "Once at thirty-two ... her daughter would be collected from the crèche" and "At six, the day before they took her"
     - Ch 13: "Maren had not touched her daughter in six years"
     - Ch 19: "once a month for six years"
   - Why both can't be true: Pell was taken at six and is now twelve, so she has been a ward for six years. That allows six or seven yearly drawings and about 72 monthly visits. Eleven drawings and 132 visits (11 × 12) both describe eleven years.
   - Severity: **minor**

2. **Where Anselm's reckoning sheet ends**
   - Chapters: 2, 17
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 2: "a single column of numbers from top to bottom of one side and halfway down the other, one line for each year, from a year marked simply *Founding* down to the present"
     - Ch 2: "until about two-thirds of the way down the second side it reached a line where Anselm had stopped writing numbers. He had drawn a box around that line ... And beneath the box, alone in the white space, one more line ... *And since?*"
     - Ch 17: "Three hundred and forty-one lines in his small upright pencil, from *Founding* to the present, and the box drawn round Year 280"
     - Ch 17 also has Ysolde supplying the later years herself: "And then she wrote the sixty-one lines after it"
   - Why both can't be true: Ch 2's main description has the sheet stop at the boxed Year 280, with blank space and "And since?" below it. Ch 17 says it runs all 341 years to the present. Ch 2 also contradicts itself:
     - It says the column ends halfway down side two, but the stopping line is two-thirds of the way down.
     - Year 280 comes before the present, so its line can't sit below the end of the column.
   - Severity: **minor**

3. **Anselm's running balance is still in the millions in the current volumes**
   - Chapters: 2 (and its own Year 280 conclusion; also 5 and 17)
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 2, of volumes 204–206 (206 being the volume in current use): "The same running figure ... Shrinking." and "This was a number in the low millions."
     - Ch 2, same sheet: "*Zero. Year 280.*"
     - Ch 2: "Sixty-one years. Sixty-one Reckonings since Year 280"
   - Why both can't be true: Anselm's pencilled balance reaches zero in Year 280, 61 years ago. In the newest volumes, which cover the last few years, the same running figure can't still be a positive balance in the low millions that the tithes are steadily reducing.
   - Severity: **minor**

4. **Age of the forged Ninth Ledger volume**
   - Chapter: 5
   - Quotes:
     - "Volume Forty-one, which covered the hundred and thirty-eighth to the hundred and forty-second years of the Count"
     - "*Rebound for preservation.* ... *The 279th year of the Count.*"
     - The new pages "had been sewn into a book that was a hundred and twenty years old at the time they were made"
     - Later in Ch 5, the forger is placed at "a table in this Nave in the two hundred and seventy-ninth year of the Count"
   - Why both can't be true: a volume finished in year 142 and altered in year 279 was about 137–141 years old, not 120.
   - Severity: **trivial**

5. **Headcount in the Hulk hall**
   - Chapters: 9, 12
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 9: "When we were all at the top, all thirty-two of them" and "Thirty-two children, a girl of nineteen, an old woman of seventy-four and me"
     - Ch 12, before anyone else has arrived: "thirty-four people breathing in a room built for none"
   - Why both can't be true: 32 children plus Wren, Haddie and Rook makes 35. Mattie is already dead, and no one else has joined or died.
   - Severity: **trivial**

6. **The Lanthorn crèche children: eleven including the baby, or eleven plus the baby**
   - Chapters: 12, 15
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 12: "Eleven. The youngest was a baby in a sling across her chest."
     - Ch 12, later: "eleven children and a baby" and "with eleven children, a baby, a sixteen-year-old girl"
     - Mother Yelland: "There's a dozen in the crèche"
     - Ch 15: "eleven from Lanthorn and a baby"
   - Why both can't be true: the first count puts the baby among the eleven. Every later count, and Yelland's "dozen", treats the baby as a twelfth child.
   - Severity: **trivial**

7. **How long Maren has lived above the Drowned Decks**
   - Chapters: 7 (against 1, 4, 10)
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 7: "In nineteen years of living above it she had never been down to the Drowned Decks"
     - Ch 4: she was born in the Sump, "you came out in the shuttle lock at Level Two". She lived in "these two rooms" with her mother before taking the debt at nineteen. The Decks flooded "forty years ago" (Ch 1).
     - Ch 10, Dov: "I've known you thirty-eight years"
   - Why both can't be true: Maren is 38 and has spent her whole life in the Sump over the flooded levels, so it has been 38 years, not 19. Nineteen is the time since she took the bond.
   - Severity: **trivial**

8. **When Rook quit as reeve**
   - Chapters: 3, 6, 15, 18
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 3, Wren at 19: "Ten years in the vents"
     - Ch 15, Jory at the tithe: "He was nine." Then: "He died two years later. Red fever ... He was eleven ... I went back to Kell the next week and told the Margrave I was done."
     - Against these, Ch 6: "since I took off the reeve's coat seven years ago"
     - Ch 15: "Seven years. Every night."
     - Ch 15, Kask: "He'd been my reeve for six years" (at the tithe)
     - Ch 3 and 6: a reeve "for nine years"
   - Why both can't be true: the tithe was 10 years ago. If Jory died two years later and Rook quit the next week, he quit 8 years ago, not 7. That also gives him 6 + 2 = 8 years as reeve, not 9. Every other figure agrees with each other; only "two years later" is out by a year.
   - Severity: **minor**

9. **Castellane says the *Grace* passes Gallows "tomorrow"**
   - Chapters: 16 (against 10, 19)
   - Quotes:
     - Ch 16, the night before departure: "When the *Grace* passes Gallows tomorrow, I was going to set the pins to invert behind her."
     - Ch 10, Dov: "The *Grace* at convoy speed, with escort ... four days"
     - Ch 19: after the *Patience*'s run of more than three and a half days, the *Grace* is "eighteen hours behind us now"
   - Why both can't be true: the *Grace* leaves at first bell the next day and needs about four days to reach Gallows. The Factor, of all people, would know it doesn't pass Gallows tomorrow.
   - Severity: **minor**

10. **Corin's "twenty years" as a Nullist**
    - Chapters: 14, 17
    - Quotes:
      - Ch 14: "I've been doing this twenty years, Aurek" and "I've been scratching circles for twenty years"
      - Ch 17: "I scratched my first circle that night ... Twenty years."
      - Ch 17, same scene: "Tamsin went down in the three hundred and twenty-sixth year." His first circle was "that night", after her death.
      - The present is "the three hundred and forty-first year" (Ch 11, 20)
    - Why both can't be true: if the first circle came the night Tamsin died, in Year 326, he has been scratching them for 15 years, not 20.
    - Severity: **minor**

11. **Ysolde's "three nights ago" at the Reliquary**
    - Chapters: 20 (against 11, 14, 17)
    - Quotes:
      - Ch 20, at Gallows: the tag she had seen "three nights ago in the dark of the Reliquary hanging from the arm of a plain metal chair"
      - The timeline in Ch 14: "on the second night", then "on the third morning", then the audit "the next day", the chapel that night, and departure after Piet's return
      - Ch 17: "It took her six days", then "the seventh night", then "On the eighth day they saw it", then arrival the next day
    - Why both can't be true: the break-in at the Reliquary was about two weeks before she reaches Gallows, not three nights.
    - Severity: **minor**

12. **Words on Maren's recording that Maren never says**
    - Chapters: 19, 20
    - Quotes:
      - Ch 20, describing the moment Quist turns white: "near the end, when the voice said *whoever hears this, if it isn't her*, and paused, and said again, more quietly, *I hope it's you, Pell*"
      - Ch 19, the recording as made. Near the start: "I hope they do. I hope it's you." (no name). Later: "Whoever hears this," she said. "If it isn't her. If it's somebody else, a long time after. The console says it can be opened again ..." That passage has no "I hope it's you, Pell".
    - Why both can't be true: the line that sets off Quist's recognition doesn't appear where Ch 20 places it, or in that wording, in the recording Ch 19 gives in full.
    - Severity: **minor**

13. **"Three hundred and forty years" since Gallows was abandoned**
    - Chapters: 17, 20 (against the chapter headers 2431 and 2794, and Ch 8)
    - Quotes:
      - Ch 17: the station door "rimed with three hundred and forty years of frost" and "nothing to show that anyone had been there in three hundred and forty years"
      - Ch 20: "A diver from the Lungs, three hundred and forty years ago" and "Three hundred and forty years in the cold and the dark"
      - Against these, the headers: Maren dies at Gallows in "*The Bond · 2431*", and Ysolde arrives in "*The Ledger · 2794*", 363 years later.
      - Ch 8 places the start of the Count, now 341 years old, after the closure. The First Auditor "was young when the Door was shut — very young" and wrote the first Count "when she was grown — when she was about your age". That gap matches 2431 + 22 = 2453 = 2794 − 341.
    - Why both can't be true: by the book's own dating, and by the Ledger era's own story of the First Auditor, the Door was shut about 22 years before the Count began. The narration nonetheless treats the Gallows dead as Count-age.
    - Severity: **minor**
