# Book P: verified continuity errors

Both audits (P-1: 14 claims; P-2: 17) merged into 20 distinct claims. Each was checked against the
text under the pre-registered rules: **accept** the book's own facts clashing, in narration or
precise facts within a scene, including a character's statement about their own life; **reject**
a character's estimate, rounding, rhetoric or belief, in-world myth, and anything ambiguous or
explainable in one line. Quotes were run through `quotes.py` first; those it could not match
(mostly quotes joined across lines with `/`, or the auditor's own paraphrase) were read in the book.
Where the auditors' severities differ, the verdict's is given, never higher than the higher of theirs.

## Accepted (14)

**Minor (7)**
1. **Hesketh's death** (P-1 #1, P-2 #1). Ch 1: Hesketh "had gone into Gall the year Abel was born", and Abel is 29. Ch 7: Hesketh "made you chief", 9 years ago; Ch 16: "taught her twenty-six years ago". Narration against narration.
2. **Maren's years on the line** (P-1 #2, P-2 #2). Ch 1: "tether-chief for nine years and on the line for twenty-six before that", which would have her start at six, since she is 41 (Ekene, 52, is "eleven years older"). Later chapters make the 26 years her whole time on the line.
3. **The Ocampo household of five** (P-1 #3, P-2 #4). Ch 2: "Household of five", and then mother, father, Abuela, Tomás, Juli and Pilar herself are named: six. It is five only after Abuela dies (Ch 20).
4. **The Lamp's closest pass on 8 August** (P-1 #8, P-2 #7). Ch 17: "three days from its closest pass", and later that day "two days"; Ch 11 and Ch 20 put the pass on 9 August.
5. **The *Wick*: 50 m or 200 m from the tug** (P-1 #9, P-2 #8). Ch 17: "fifty metres from where Corin set the *Wick* down"; Ch 20: "two hundred metres from a tug", with the skiff not moved.
6. **Ama Venn's "nine days"** (P-1 #10, P-2 #9). She is in South Lock, not yet out, on 31 July (Ch 14), and died "the sixth" after "Nine days without a stop" (Ch 20). A precise count, not hedged.
7. **"Five weeks" since the Throat** (P-1 #11, P-2 #10). Narration in Ch 21; 12 April (Ch 6) to 2 May is under three weeks.

**Trivial (7)**
8. **The egg's stencil: three lines or four** (P-1 #5, P-2 #14). Isaura sees "three lines" (Ch 17) and broadcasts "four lines stencilled on it" (Ch 20).
9. **Abel's drilling** (P-1 #7, P-2 #11). Ch 16: "landed at the ninth hour", "It took him six hours", "At the thirteenth the auger stopped".
10. **The siege calendar** (P-1 #12, P-2 #6). Day 18 is 30 June (Ch 5, Ch 8), so "eight days into July" cannot be "twenty-eight days into the siege" (Ch 8).
11. **The air floor** (P-1 #13, P-2 #5). Five live on "Two hundred for five" (Ch 5), yet for one prisoner "Nobody can live on ninety" (Ch 11).
12. **Abel's height** (P-1 #14). "had just grown taller than her" at sixteen (Ch 4), but "the same height as her" as an adult and "always been the same height" (Ch 16).
13. **The second T** (P-2 #12). Ch 13: "MAREN TSOLO … the awl skidded on the second T"; the name has one T.
14. **"Two words"** (P-2 #13). Ch 20: "Above the line, two words. *WE SENT IT ROUND*": four.

## Rejected (6)

- **The drum hall's eleven metres** (P-1 #4). Against "the size of a cathedral", a simile in narration: rhetoric.
- **Dasha's twelve years on the Ninth North** (P-1 #6). She grew up there and has lived there twelve years: explainable in one line (she left and came back).
- **Six marines become eight** (P-2 #3). The marine taped in the cold hold and the one surrendering at the collar may be among the "Four marines" later held in the crew deck, which with the sergeant and the dead one makes six: explainable.
- **The fur collar in the Council** (P-2 #15). Isaura's recollection: a character's memory.
- **Where Abel was, at three, the night their mother died** (P-2 #16). "carried on her hip through the third shaft when their mother went into the cloud" need not be that same night: ambiguous.
- **"Nobody unshared" has left Kettle** (P-2 #17). The narrator's generalisation: a character's belief.

**Book P: 14 verified (serious 0, minor 7, trivial 7).**
