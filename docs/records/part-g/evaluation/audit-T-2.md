# Continuity audit: book-T

Chronology I used to check these: Voyage V opened at Callis about two years before CR 611. Ottilie Brask died the night before "twelve days before the Burning". The Night of the Hulk is CR 929. The Salvage is CR 1317, 388 years later. Hollis Fen is 41 (lifted at 12, 29 years ago). Yara Tull is 24.

1. **The number of Ordinances left unrecited**
   - Chapters: 1
   - Quotes: The plaque holds "twenty-two articles". Annick recites Articles One, Four, Nine, Eleven, Fourteen and Twenty. Benn then says: "You don't need the other seventeen tonight."
   - Why they conflict: 22 minus the six she recited is 16, not 17.
   - Severity: trivial

2. **Gallows Reach is deeper than its glacier**
   - Chapters: 2 (also 19)
   - Quotes: "they dug the first galleries with hand tools into the face of a glacier eight hundred metres thick" / "The Haskells lived near the bottom. Eleven hundred metres down". Chapter 19 says of Tamar: "Eight hundred metres of it at the thinnest, then a sea."
   - Why they conflict: The town is described as going "down into the ice like a set of stairs". A gallery 1,100 m down cannot be inside an 800 m glacier.
   - Severity: minor

3. **Benn's age when Voyage V opened**
   - Chapters: 1, 4 (with 10 and 19)
   - Quotes: Chapter 1: "He was seventy-one". Benn himself says "I'm seventy-one, Annick". Chapter 4, listening to the opening entry: "He'd been sixty, opening this voyage."
   - Why they conflict: That would make the voyage eleven years old. But Marit's boarding entry is "an entry from two years ago" (ch. 10), and Abena is "Age seven" at boarding and "Nine years old" in ch. 19. Dace, now 22, boarded at the voyage's opening. Benn would have been about 69, not 60.
   - Severity: serious

4. **"Three hundred in the Wake" is more than the Wake holds**
   - Chapters: 1, 4, 10, 13
   - Quotes: "two hundred and forty awake" (ch. 1) and "two hundred and forty waking" (ch. 4) / "There were three hundred of them in the Wake who would move when Tobiah said move" (ch. 4). Chapter 10 repeats "the three hundred in the Wake", and ch. 13 has "Tobiah's three hundred". Chapter 10 also gives "the whole rotation ... two hundred and thirty bonded".
   - Why they conflict: The Wake is the bonded who are awake, and the sleepers are counted separately ("There were more in the Hold, in the cradles, who could be woken"). So 300 of the Wake cannot exceed the 230 to 240 people in it.
   - Severity: minor

5. **Ottilie Brask's age**
   - Chapters: 2, 5
   - Quotes: Chapter 2, when Hollis was twelve: "She'd been fifty-four then" (Hollis has known her "for twenty-nine years") / Chapter 5, Marsh: "She was sixty-three."
   - Why they conflict: 54 plus 29 is 83. If she was 63, she was 34 when Hollis was twelve.
   - Severity: minor

6. **The day the verifier was due**
   - Chapters: 2, 5
   - Quotes: Chapter 2, on the day "twelve days before the Burning": "The Concordance verifier arrives in two days" / Chapter 5, on the morning "ten days before the Burning": "There's something else. The verifier docks at eleven." "I thought he was due tomorrow." "He made good time."
   - Why they conflict: Two days after day 12 is day 10. He arrives on the day he was due, not a day early.
   - Severity: trivial

7. **Ottilie's cipher examples do not fit the Catechism as given**
   - Chapters: 2, 5 (also 20)
   - Quotes: The Catechism recited in ch. 2 runs "Who carried us? / The Increase carried us. / Who would have sold us? ..." with line 7 "Who saved us?". Chapter 5: "*Three-one-one* was *W*, from *What*. *Seven-four-two* was *U*, from *our*." Chapter 20, Wren: "Line seven ... Word four."
   - Why they conflict: In the text as given, line 3 begins "Who", not "What". Line 7 has only three words, so it has no fourth word. "our" is in line 12. This only holds if the ch. 2 recitation is the full text, which it appears to be.
   - Severity: trivial

8. **Calla was on the Deep Ice when Jory was struck**
   - Chapters: 5, 14
   - Quotes: Chapter 5: "She'd done four years down there, for her father's portion. She'd come back up and organised the Hold in the six years since." Jory was struck "Seven years ago". Chapter 14, Calla: "He borrowed them the week before ... I didn't speak to him for three days. Then you came." Also: "My mother sold it the year Jory was struck."
   - Why they conflict: On the natural reading she was on the Deep Ice from about ten years ago to six years ago. That covers Jory's strike seven years ago, yet the boots story has her at home that week.
   - Severity: minor

9. **The copper bar count**
   - Chapters: 3
   - Quotes: Before the slosh: "They are three bars up and two hours from the end of their air". After it: "I lost a bar." "We've got three." "We had four."
   - Why they conflict: If they had three before the slosh and lost one, they would have two left. If they had four, the earlier line is wrong.
   - Severity: trivial

10. **The age of the Widow's weld**
    - Chapters: 3, 11
    - Quotes: Chapter 3 (1317): "welded at the rim with a bead of slag three centuries old" / Chapter 11 (929): the same hatch is already "welded all the way round with a thick dull bead of slag, very old ... *Fourth Cistern. Closed CR 718.*"
    - Why they conflict: The weld existed and was old by 929, so in 1317 it is at least 388 years old and probably about 600. It cannot be three centuries old. Chapter 3 also says Gorse re-welded it last spring. The Salvage narration often rounds 388 years to "three hundred", but 600 is too far off for that.
    - Severity: trivial

11. **How long Hollis kept the brass instruments**
    - Chapters: 8 (with 2 and 5)
    - Quotes: "a set of Charter-era brass instruments I'd bought in a Saltmarch junk shop at twenty and kept on my shelf for nineteen years"
    - Why they conflict: Hollis is 41: lifted at 12 and known to Marsh "for twenty-nine years", and inside the vault at thirteen, "twenty-eight years" ago. Bought at twenty, he has had them 21 years, not 19.
    - Severity: trivial

12. **Rask's rounds**
    - Chapters: 14 (also 17)
    - Quotes: "I do the rounds at ten and twelve and two." / "I came back on the twelve o'clock round. Half past eleven, it was, when I got to the grille. I'm slow these days."
    - Why they conflict: Arriving at 11:30 on the twelve o'clock round is early, not late, so "I'm slow these days" does not explain it.
    - Severity: trivial

13. **Three days go missing before the Burning**
    - Chapters: 11, 14, 20
    - Quotes: Chapter 11: "at nine in the morning, six days before the Burning" (that day: the vault, the arrest, the escape). Chapter 14 the same night: "I'd been a free man for about eleven hours", then: "The next day was the day before the day before the Burning". Chapter 20: "I'd floated past it again six days ago with Thorne."
    - Why they conflict: The day after day 6 is day 5, not day 2. Three days vanish, yet ch. 20 still counts the vault visit as six days before the Burning.
    - Severity: minor

14. **The count of the Burning**
    - Chapters: 8, 14
    - Quotes: Chapter 8 (CR 929): "three hundred and eighteen years ago, on the first Burning Day" / Chapter 14 caption: "the celebration of the three hundred and eighteenth Burning".
    - Why they conflict: If the first Burning was in CR 611, the one in 929 is the 319th.
    - Severity: trivial

15. **How long Bastian kept silent**
    - Chapters: 2, 17
    - Quotes: Ottilie died the night before Hollis's "twelve days before the Burning". Chapter 17, on Burning Day, Bastian speaks "as if it had been waiting behind his teeth for nine days."
    - Why they conflict: From the murder to the Burning is about twelve and a half days by the book's own day labels.
    - Severity: trivial

16. **The ring is gone, yet she swims down it**
    - Chapters: 18
    - Quotes: "The stump of the ring is gone entirely." / A few lines later: "The Mouth is still there. She goes in. Down the ring to the forward spoke."
    - Why they conflict: She cannot travel down a ring that the same passage says is entirely gone.
    - Severity: trivial

17. **How long the bonded have been in the dark**
    - Chapters: 10, 19
    - Quotes: Chapter 10: Marit's boarding at Callis is "an entry from two years ago", and Abena goes from "Age seven" to "Nine years old" (ch. 19). / Chapter 19, Annick: "They've been in the dark for fourteen months".
    - Why they conflict: The bonded have been aboard since boarding at Callis, so their time in the dark cannot be both two years and fourteen months.
    - Severity: minor

18. **Hollis quotes things Annick's entry never says**
    - Chapters: 16, 19, 20
    - Quotes: Chapter 20, describing her recorded entry: "*Liberty*, which is the word Edwin Quarry chose, she says, in the captain's day room at the Fork, with a glass of the captain's whisky in front of him. *Call it something they can love.*" Also: "the way the bonded did in the Crossing, she says, to save the air. Four in. Hold. Four out."
    - Why they conflict: The entry in ch. 19 is given in full; she reads it "All of it. From *Entry* to *each other*". It says only that Szell, Ruck and Quarry "agreed among themselves ... to call it Liberty". It has no day room, no whisky, no "something they can love" (words Annick overheard in ch. 16 and never recorded), and nothing about counting breaths. Hollis could not have learned these from the Book.
    - Severity: minor

19. **The recovery-port plate is smaller than its shaft**
    - Chapters: 19, 20, 21
    - Quotes: "a round iron plate a metre across with eight bolts round its rim" (ch. 19, ch. 20, ch. 21) / directly beneath it: "A shaft. Two metres across" (ch. 20, ch. 21).
    - Why they conflict: A plate one metre across cannot cover a shaft two metres across. In ch. 21 it even "falls through its own frame" into that shaft.
    - Severity: trivial

Things I checked and found consistent, so did not list:
- Callis is "eleven jumps" from Absalom: 8 jumps to Pell, 1 to the Fork, 2 down the Absalom branch.
- The Fork timing (35 days, revised to 17).
- The perigee and pass counts in 1317, including "Ten days" and "twenty-one days".
- Hollis's age and strike count (308, then 309).
- Wren's birthday countdown.
- Key numbers 0001 and 0447, and the missing Voyage III box.
- The Fourth Cistern closed in CR 718.
- The 388-year interest span.
- The Saal debt split.
- Annick's "fifteen years" of sending money home.

I did not count these as errors, because the book presents them as in-world beliefs or reveals:
- The legend of Fen the Turncoat.
- Yara taking Marsh's body for Fen's.
- "The Purser would have sold us".
- The Ash Case being the Voyage III book.

Counts: serious 1, minor 7, trivial 11 (total 19).
