You are a working novelist. Write a complete novel. The book is the whole job.

## The book

A **gritty space opera in 21 chapters**.
- **Three eras.** It spans three distinct eras separated by centuries, with completely different characters in each timeline.
- **Interlaced, not in blocks.** The chapters are not told in chronological blocks. They interlace, weaving together one overarching narrative.
- **Anchors (optional).** Specific items or locations may subtly reappear across the centuries to anchor the world.
- **The hard part: synchronized pacing.**
  - The future timelines must not spoil the past.
  - Although the eras are separated by history, their narrative arcs unfold simultaneously for the reader.
  - All three timelines build toward their respective climaxes at the same pace, without revealing each other's secrets or endings prematurely.
- **Your choices:** you decide everything else. That includes the premise, the eras, the people, places, items, history, knowledge (who knows what, and when the reader learns it), relationships, factions, plot threads, calendar and structure.
- **Length:** write a real, complete novel you would stand behind, with full prose for every scene, not outlines.

- each chapter starts with a line `# Chapter N: Title`;
- next comes a line in italics naming the chapter's era and year, e.g. `*Era name · year*`;
- then the prose;
- scenes within a chapter are separated by a line `* * *`;
- no other headings, notes or commentary inside `book.md`.
- To make `book.md`, export the manuscript from the app through its own UI (Markdown is easiest) and bring it into this format with a script. Make sure nothing but the book's prose is left in it: no scene headers or app markup.
