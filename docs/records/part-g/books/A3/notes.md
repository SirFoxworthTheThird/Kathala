# WHAT THE DARK IS OWED — working notes

## World
- Sabra system (dim K star) beyond the Throat (wormhole to the Concord). Gas giant Nhai; ice moon Caul (mine: Caul Deep).
- Lantern: hollow nickel-iron asteroid habitat spun for gravity; axial fusion lamp "the Wick". Counting House (Tally vault), Dock Ring with the Bell Hook, Galleries (worker housing), Pumphouse (air plant), Upper Wards.
- The Tally: Halden-Ash Company ledger engine; every lock, pump, scrubber keyed to it; debits air/water/heat, credits labour. Negative past line = "dimmed".
- Collapsed throats heal in ~700 years (known physics).
- Calendar: CE; AC (After Closing) begins after 2387 CE. Era III = 3089 CE = 702 AC.

## Eras
- I  The Closing — 2387 CE (ch 1,4,7,10,13,16,19)
- II The Owing — 311 AC (ch 2,5,8,11,14,17,20)
- III The Healing — 3089 CE (ch 3,6,9,12,15,18,21)
