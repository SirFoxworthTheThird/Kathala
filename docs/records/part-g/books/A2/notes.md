## The Hush (Asset Seven; Sanitation Agent, Pattern H)
Ceramic egg ~70cm, 40kg, cryo sleeve (~20 K). Fail-deadly shell: breach or warming above 40 K vents payload.
Agent kills humans in 6-10 hrs (they go quiet), spares machines. Dormant spores in vacuum forever.
Destroyed only by sustained extreme pressure + heat (deep of a giant). "You'd have to get it there cold, faster than it can wake." (Quist, ch10)
Dropping it in Gall in 1391 = it would wake on entry in the skim layer → poison the Bucket intake → the colony.
Tamsin's Rock: company Hushed it; 4,000 dead; Ekene was contract medic after.
