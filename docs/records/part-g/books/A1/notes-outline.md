# WHAT THE NEEDLE KEEPS — working outline (writer's notes)

## World
- The Needle: natural wormhole, Reach-side mouth ~2M km from Hollin. Transit ~9h through the throat (shifting geometry; needs a throat-reader + pilot). Inward mouth near Port Ardent.
- Breathes: every 4,000 days a Narrowing (throat tightens 30-40h, impassable "Long Night"). Depth follows a slow beat; 2291 is a deep trough.
- Brine-glass: exotic-matter crystal from Hollin's deep salt. Burst risk, glass-lung.
- Hollin: moon of gas giant Caul. Tallow (town/city; later "Tallow Under"), Shaft Nine, Lantern Row (bar: the Lantern), the Pans (salt flats), the Gutter (Era III slum ring).
- Anchorage Station (I) -> the Anchorhold (II, Wardens' fortress-cloister) -> the Anchor (III, half-dark). The Lectern = observatory with the harp (throat-reading instrument: tensioned resonator strings singing to the throat's noise).
- The Shoals: outer asteroid habitats.
- The Ossuary (III): graveyard of Returns near the mouth.
- The buoy ("the Bell" in III): Ambrose's sounding buoy dropped by Teo in ch4; has a cradle for the fork.

## The lie
- 2291: Hesper Vane uses deep Narrowing to found the Keeping (crewed tugs inject brine-glass at the throat's waist "to feed it"). Truth: Narrowing natural; recovers on its own. Keeping = governance instrument (Vane Memorandum). Injected glass ACCRETES (scar); ~60 Keepings choke it (Ambrose, ch16).
- After Ambrose's death Hesper detunes the harp to the "Hunger Tone" (masks the natural recovery), keeps his fork as relic ("For clarity"; engraving "for Clare" worn -> read "for Cl..." = "For Clarity").
- Hesper becomes First Warden. Each High Warden inherits the Testament (the Inheritance).
- Era II: Draw every 4000 days, nine Keepers; Draw is rigged (Shoal names weighted; some chosen). Mor chose Pell to coerce Speaker Efua Asante.
- Era III: throat choked by six centuries of glass; Returns = throat expelling Keeper ships. Ringing at true pitch via buoy + harp clears it.
