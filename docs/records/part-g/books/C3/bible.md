# SURETY — series bible

## One-line
A debt imposed in 2431, a door shut to save children, a faith built on the lie of that debt, a woman who audits the lie and leaves a receipt at the door, and a man who presents the receipt when the creditor finally comes.

## The system
- Star: **Vigil**, a red dwarf. Light is red/bruise-coloured everywhere.
- **Tarrow**: gas giant. Deep red layers yield **heavy breath** (metastable condensate; fuels Throat drives and the pins). Divers ("breathers") dive skimmers into it. "The rattle" = breathers' lung disease.
- **Hesper**: ice moon. Bonds' warren dug into its crust = **the Sump** (Hesper Low). Flooded lower galleries = **the Drowned Decks**.
- **Hesper High**: Combine station in Hesper orbit: Nursery (crèche), the Residence (Factor), docks. -> Era II **the Nave** (Auditory seat) -> Era III **the Hulk** (dead ruin).
- **Kell**: tidally heated rocky moon. Era III refinery **Kell Deep**.
- **The Throat** (Era I) / **the Door** (II, III): natural wormhole at Tarrow's trailing Trojan point. Held open by four **pins** (slang: "the Hanged Men") controlled from **Gallows** station. Region around: **the Shear** (tidal turbulence), old Combine **picket mines**.
- The **Gallows Key**: a finger-long spindle of ceramic and steel, Factor's authority token for pin commands (OPEN / HOLD / INVERT / RESTORE). Inversion collapses the Throat to a thread in minutes.
- **Account HV-VIGIL-0001**: the Combine's colony account. Era II: "the Holy Number" heading every ledger page. Era III: in the herald's notice.

## Anchors
- Gallows (all three climaxes).
- Hesper High -> Nave -> Hulk. The Reliquary = (secretly) the hull of *Halloran's Grace*, grafted into the station. Never identified before ch20. Ch11 describes "ribbed vault, curved walls"; ch18 Rook sees a ribbed vault.
- The Gallows Key.
- Surety tags: steel disc broken on a jagged line; parent keeps one half, ward the other. "When the debt's paid the halves are matched." Era II divers wear tags as charms (origin forgotten). First Auditor's relic in the Reliquary: half a disc, stamping kissed smooth (ch11). Era III: tithe-tags (Wren LAN-9-0437).
- Saying: "Count what's owed and not a mark more" (breathers' saying, Era I common; Era II carved over Ledger Hall, attributed to First Auditor; Era III garbled: "count it and not a mark more").
- Breathers' hymn, drifting:
  I: "Down in the red where the weight comes on, / down where the light's a bruise, / bring up the breath for the ones who are owed / and bring yourself up if you choose."
  II: same but "for the ones who are owed" -> sung as "for the Count that is owed".
  III: fragments.
- Three taps on glass = "still here" (Maren & Pell).

## ERA I — The Bond · 2431 (close third, Maren; past tense; muscular)
- **Maren Tull**, 38, skimmer diver, bond H-11873; inherited mother's debt (mother Ada died of the rattle). Skimmer *Gannet*.
- **Pell Tull**, 12, surety ward 0212, in the Nursery since 6. Dry humour, counts everything.
- **Dov Arkwright**, 55, tender pilot of *Old Patience*; flew for Maren's mother; drinker, rattle. Sells the union's dock plan for a seat on the Grace (ch13 betrayal, revealed ch16). Combine reneges. Redeems: flies Maren to Gallows, decoys *Indemnity*, dies ch19.
- **Sister Agathe Hatch**, 64, clinic nurse, union organiser. Captured ch13, "liquidated" (spaced) on broadcast ch16.
- **Lucan Vey**, 27, junior actuary, Factor's office; queried ward valuations, punished by being named to the caretaker detail of twelve who will stay behind. Leaks. Dies ch16 helping steal the key.
- **Factor Imre Castellane**, 60, Combine Factor. Key on chain at his throat. "Without the ledger there is only the knife." Mentions Recovery ch7. Trapped on the Grace at the end -> (secretly) chief Steward.
- Matron **Odile Brask** (Nursery). Captain **Hale Rennick** (*Indemnity*). Escorts *Indemnity*, *Surety*. Flagship *Halloran's Grace*.
- Combine plan (Exodus Clause): Vigil unprofitable; pins failing anyway. Evacuate all Combine staff + breath stockpile + 412 surety contracts ("liquid assets", to be sold to Brannock Deepworks, Corvid system) on the Grace. Leave twelve caretakers "to keep the account until Recovery." Grace stops at Gallows; Castellane keys a timed inversion; Throat closes behind them. Bonds left.
- Public story: "the Scholarship" — selected wards to Combine academies.
- Timeline: D1 ch1 (convoy D40) · D3 ch4 · D9 ch7 · D18-19 ch10 (strike; convoy moved to D23) · D22 ch13 (farewell/dock fight) · D23 ch16 (Hatch killed; key stolen; Lucan dies; Dov exposed; launch) · D23-26 ch19 (Tarrow slingshot; Gallows; inversion; Maren dies).
- *Surety* escort goes through ahead (escapes; reports collapse -> Combine sends Recovery receiver).

## ERA II — The Ledger · 2794 (close third, Ysolde; past; measured, numerate)
- Society ~190,000. The **Auditory** rules from **the Nave**. Count Board (flip-digits) in the Ledger Hall: ~3.9 billion marks. Labour tithe logged as payment to HV-VIGIL-0001. "Interest of Sorrow" added each Reckoning.
- Catechism: What are we? The owed. What do we owe? All that was spent to bring us. Why is the Door shut? Because the account is open. When will it open? When the Count is zero. Who keeps the Count? The Auditors, for the Stewards, for the Combine.
- Lore: the Door was shut against us; the **Twelve Stewards** were "left to keep the account"; the **Starving** (first decades); the **First Auditor** "learned her numbers at the Stewards' knees" and wrote the first Count; her name is spoken only in the Prelate's oath (secret: Pell Tull).
- The **Lungs** = divers' quarter (old Sump). "Sent to the Breath" = execution in refinery intake. **Nullists** = heretics, sign a circle.
- **Ysolde Marr**, 31, Junior Auditor, Third Chancery; Lungs-born, "taken up" at 9 for aptitude. Brother **Piet**, 22, diver.
- **Brother Anselm Ferrow**, 68, keeper of the Ninth Ledger; murdered ch2.
- **Corin Asch**, 44, ex-diver, breath smuggler, Nullist. Boat *Small Mercy*. Daughter Tamsin died at 14 diving to pay her share. Dies ch17.
- **Prelate-Auditor Aurel Quist**, 71, mentor; believes the Count is a necessary binding; knows Interest is fiction; does not know Gallows truth. Holds the Steward's Ring (secret: Castellane's factor signet, H-V mark, with authority chip) — not described until ch20.
- **Bailiff-Captain Mattias Strand**.
- Stewards' Compact (First Book): "The Door is closed and shall be taught as closed against them... No soul shall go to Gallows. What was done at Gallows is accounted and closed. Let the Count bind them, for nothing else will." (twelve brown signatures, illegible)
- Plot: ch2 Anselm dead; his note "Zero, Year 302. And since?" + Nullist tag + "Asch. Lungs. Interest of Sorrow." · ch5 Interest is a forged insertion; without it Count zeroed Y302; meets Corin; Bailiff raid; summons from Quist · ch8 dinner/catechism; Piet's arrears; Ysolde alters ledgers; learns of First Book · ch11 Reliquary break-in; First Book: Founding figure smaller; Compact; Gallows; WARDS page glimpsed unread; flight; declared in default · ch14 Piet arrested; public Audit; trade pages for Piet; decides on Gallows; launch with Corin + breath · ch17 nine days; writes audit by hand; Corin's story; the Shear; Strand's cutter; Corin dies; alone docks Gallows · ch20 Maren's body; tag H-11873; log; restart pin (RESTORE, lowest — centuries); types audit into console; Quist & Strand arrive; Quist hears "Pell"; Strand tries to blow pins; Quist shot; Quist seals the closing entry with the ring; names Ysolde successor before the surviving Bailiffs; dies. Ysolde lays him beside Maren, leaves paper receipt + note in tin, takes Maren's half-tag to bring to the First Auditor's chair; goes back to do the slow thing.

## ERA III — The Door · 3190 (FIRST PERSON, Absalom Rook; past; sardonic, guilty)
- ~40,000 people. Habitats: **Lanthorn** (lashed hulks in Tarrow's ring shadow), **Kell Deep** refinery, the **Hulk**, Sallow, the Kettles. The Margrave controls breath.
- **The Tithe**: one child in ten at nine to Kell Deep vents. Reeves collect. Tags.
- The Door has been "breathing" (widening) 31 years. Door-watchers.
- **Absalom Rook**, 52, ex-reeve, picker, boat *Mendicant*. Wife Marta left; son **Jory** died at 11 of red fever. Secret: when Jory's lot came up, Rook put Wren's name in his place.
- **Wren**, 19, LAN-9-0437, ten years in Kell Deep. Burn-scarred hands. Mother Lise died while she was inside.
- **Haddie Coyle**, 74, door-watcher, reads "ledger hand" (old script). Dies ch18.
- **Margrave Teodora Kask**: wants to be the creditor's factor; later wants the Door shut to stay queen.
- **The Remittance**: Combine Recovery Receiver, waited 760 years beyond the Door. Herald (black seed-shaped beacon), tenders, main hull too big until the Door widens. Voice: courteous, flat.
- Herald notice (ch6): "NOTICE OF RECOVERY. Account HV-VIGIL-0001 is in arrears. A receiver will attend upon the reopening of the passage. All assets will be inventoried and recovered. Debtors may present statements of payment under seal of an authorised officer." (Haddie notes the clause; Rook laughs.)
- Plot: ch3 Wren arrives with stolen herald, knife at Rook · ch6 translation; Margrave's hunters; flee Lanthorn; Margrave will "treat with the Creditor" · ch9 raid Kell Deep, free ~30 tithed children, child dies, hide in Hulk (ribbed vault, rotted ledgers, statue of a woman with a book) · ch12 tenders through Door; Lanthorn seized; Margrave escorts; Remittance designates Hulk as Combine property/collection depot; Haddie: Gallows holds the Door · ch15 Rook out for breath; Hulk raided; Wren & kids taken; Rook goes to Kask to trade; Kask tells Wren about the swap; Rook beaten, held · ch18 Haddie springs him, dies; Rook frees Wren & kids from barge at Hulk; confession; kids sent with Lanthorn survivors; Rook & Wren to Gallows; Kask follows · ch21 Gallows: Maren + Quist, key, tin; Ysolde's note; Kask arrives; fight; main hull transiting; Rook transmits sealed closing; ACCOUNT CLOSED; refund: title to the receiver vessel; Kask tries to INVERT; Wren stops her; Rook turns key to OPEN. Wren goes out through the Door with others; Rook stays at Gallows to hold it.
