# Outline — 21 chapters, strict rotation E1/E2/E3

Pacing bands: 1–3 hook | 4–6 complication | 7–9 commitment | 10–12 midpoint secret | 13–15 escalation & loss | 16–18 dark moment | 19–21 climaxes.

1. E1 Sixty-One Days (Day 239). Ring shift, torque count. Kostya's clip fails; Lusk locks Patience; tether chain short; Kostya turns his radio off. Fenn docks the suit across the gang: 4 days each — Sol 57→61. Pell arrives, Kostya's bunk. Sol scratches marks. Flask.
2. E2 The Lottery (60 days before Firstlight, 336 AL). Idris's dawn rite in the Spindle gallery; the Knot behind its grille; Rule; Sallow. Levy Day in Basilica Square; Lark drawn; red cord. Night: Lark sings fragments; "everybody's brother says that."
3. E3 The Count for the Dead (419). Pim's funeral; Tally sings the Count down. Hold Fourteen; Quill; marks on the wall; rot; scrubbers. Ness back from a walk: the Halo is knocking. Reeve's ship Patience arrives at the Hold's dock.
4. E1 The Skirt (Day ~247). Teaching Pell the Count and the governor dogs at the Spindle. Teague coughing. Gang Nine pulled off skirt; Oyelaran. Ore-hold meeting; Sol declines. PA: Lighting advanced to Day 288 (phase instability, certified Haddad).
5. E2 The Price of a Name (~48 before). Sallow refuses. Ferrant Oake: falsify the Clemency's transit mass. Idris follows Lark to the Sump; children skipping the full rhyme with lock verses; Mother Rook; the flask "TTH-9 · 0417".
6. E3 Tribute. The Reeve in the Hold; wants the Count; Quill sings it false; deadline. Quill's two rules; lineage; she dies. Tally is the Tally — alone, breaking rule two.
7. E1 Insured Losses (Day ~258). Castellane's office; Auditor Lind reading; lies about hab ring rating. Pell burned; infirmary; indemnity schedule "Loss event projected D288." Teague: "insured against us dying." Sol corners Haddad: "Not here."
8. E2 Substitution (~36). Idris records Clemency as ore barge (true mass ten times). Lark's cord cut; Benno Kask named. Lark's fury in the bunkroom with the marks. Idris meets Benno and his mother; can't speak. Night: armored figures at the Factor's berth.
9. E3 Water. Scrubber failure; Veska trades Tally for water; Ness's rage. Aboard Patience: Combine plate; Brother Cinder in chains; the Reeve's reasons (Brine's ice failing; rot). Tally bargains for scrubbers.
10. E1 First Light Is a Wave (Day ~261–266). Haddad's confession: fake instability, policy lapses Day 290, Mercy reassigned, Assessor leaves 287, Spindle armed by internal timer on 286. Ore-hold: Sol speaks; plan to take the command deck and keep officers aboard. Teague tells Sol about Dov's sixteen reports.
11. E2 The Instrument (~24). Sallow puts Idris on the Firstlight rota; reveals the Instrument of Consolidation (fleet; Total Assessment). Sallow's acquiescence. Idris to the Sump: the Unlevied, Gaunt; Lark: "Now you see it."
12. E3 Brine. Under-ice city. The Reeve's listening room: the knock, artificial, strengthening. "Whoever owns the door owns what comes through it." Cinder: the Founders returning. Tally can't place the rhythm. Ness among the hired hullwalkers.
13. E1 Command Deck (Day 285). The rising, betrayed; Proctors waiting; spine corridor fight; gas. Officers escape to the Assessor (Castellane, Lind, Lusk); Haddad stays. Teague dies. Juno exposed, left behind; crowd wants to space her; Sol stops it. Castellane: "The Spindle is armed. I'm sorry." Sol: "Gang Four, suit up."
14. E2 The Knot (~12). Unlevied plan: seize the Spindle at Firstlight, threaten to wreck the Eye. Idris opens the grille at night; the Knot is a rigger's dog; star of ten bolts on a plate; hums "three for the dog". Sallow catches him: the Rule, the fear: "holds it up or holds it down." Dagny Shrike and the plan to smuggle Benno.
15. E3 Kettle-Weather. Expedition on Patience; Lamplighter zealots attack; Cinder torn; ship crippled, lands on the Halo far from the Spindle. Ness: the scrubbers never came. Tally's fury and resolve.
16. E1 The Dog (Day 286–287). Patience to the Spindle, Haddad unlocks drive. Seat the construction dog in the governor. Ultimatum. Lind sends Proctors. Vacuum fight; Bracken dies; Sol cuts a Proctor's tether. Pell saves her. Terms: workers into the Assessor; Sol stays at the Spindle as guarantee. She accepts.
17. E2 Firstlight Eve. Benno extraction fails; raid on the bunkroom (Oake sold Idris). Rook shot; gives flask. Lark taken, put on the Remittance with Benno. Severs' offer. Sallow's key and escape. Dagny. Patience toward the Spindle.
18. E3 The Halo. The crossing. Keepers' dogs. Kettle storm. Marrow dies. Cinder walks out to draw the dogs. Tally overhears/learns the Reeve will kill them after. Ness's suit breach. The Spindle's door; Reeve's gun on Ness.
19. E1 Lock the Door (Day 287–288). Evacuation; Pell gets the flask; Juno; Haddad tells Sol of the third notch. Alone at the Spindle: dog to neutral by the third notch; marks; ten bolts; scratches message and verses; sings over open channel; the hold sings back. Castellane's last talk. Haddad: "governor clear." First light inside the housing. Ledger coda.
20. E2 The Quench (Firstlight 337). Into the Spindle; Sallow covers. Ten bolts; Sol's plate. Lark on the Keepers' channel from the Remittance: let us through. Two thousand singing. Remittance clears. Fleet enters. Lock. The Eye closes. City dark. Idris scratches. Leaves flask. Walks down into the dark and sings to children, counting it down.
21. E3 Count It Back Down. Inside: flask, plate, three hands. Lineage realized. Knock = the Count counted up. The Reeve's relay; Tally sings the key to everyone; warns them behind the steel; the Reeve goes to the window. Count it back down. First light. A voice: "One for the hook—" "—and two for the line."
