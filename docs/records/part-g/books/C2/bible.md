# COUNT IT BACK DOWN — series bible

## Spine
One door (the gate "the Ring" / "the Eye" / "the Halo") at the brown dwarf Kettle, and who holds its key.
- Era 1 builds it, and a rigger secretly builds a lock into it that works from this side only.
- Era 2 uses the lock: a Keeper quenches the gate to stop an annexation fleet, at the cost of his sister (on the Levy ship, gone Inward) and his city (Tithe goes dark).
- Era 3, 419 years into the Dark, a Tally (rememberer) unlocks it — after making the key common knowledge — because someone on the far side is knocking in the rhythm of the Count.
The key is a song: the Rigger's Count (a torque-sequence chant). It drifts across centuries.

## Calendar
- Era 1 "The Combine": Fiscal Year 2291, days numbered. Lighting = Day 288. Sol's bond ends Day 300. Insurance (construction-loss policy, Ostrava Mutual) lapses Day 290 — that's why the Lighting is moved up.
- Era 2 "The Concord": years After Lighting (AL). Story runs Levy Day (60 days before Firstlight) of 336 AL to Firstlight (New Year) 337 AL. Firstlight = anniversary of the Lighting.
- Era 3 "The Dark": Year 419 of the Dark. The Dark began Firstlight 337 AL (NOT revealed to reader until ch20/21). Era 3 lore about how the Dark began is divided.

## Places
- Hearth: dim red dwarf, the system star. Kettle: brown dwarf, magenta-banded, storms ("Kettle-weather" = flares). Moons: Gall (rock, airless), Brine (ice, subsurface ocean), Hesk (small).
- The Ring: ~38 km across, orbiting Kettle. Inner face laced with phase-lines. The Spindle: a 900 m tower-node on the rim; holds the phase governor (a great geared ring with dog-bores; "third notch" = original quench position, removed from design at the Auditor's order but the notch remains). Inner housing: thick steel chamber. Housing plate: held by ten bolts in a star pattern (= the Count's numbers).
- TTH-9 ("Tithe"): construction platform. Hab Ring C, Bay 14 = Gang Four bunkroom (Sol scratches 61 day-marks on the wall). Ore hold = meeting place. Admin deck. Spine corridor.
- Era 2 Tithe: a city of nine million grown around the platform along the Ring. Spokes. Upper wards (Lightwardens, House factors), middle wards, the Sump (the old platform hab rings, oldest decks, bond-families). Basilica of the Light (on the old admin deck). Keepers' Spoke to the Spindle. The Mantle: city shielding ("behind the steel"). Lark's Unlevied meet in the old bunkroom (scratched marks on the wall — a Sump luck-charm).
- Era 3 Tithe: frozen ruin; Holds in shielded old districts. Hold Fourteen = the old Bay C-14 area (stencil "C-14" still on walls). Tallies' chamber has the marks ("the First Count"). The Halo (dead Ring). The Spindle forbidden; Keeper automata ("the Keepers' dogs") still patrol. Brine: under-ice city of the Reeve.

## The Count
Original work chant (E1, chief calls the number, gang answers):
  One for the hook and two for the line,
  three for the dog and four for the time,
  five for the air and six for the seal,
  seven the hand on the cold steel wheel,
  eight for the dark and nine for the drop,
  ten for the Company — ten, and stop.
  Count it up and count it slow,
  count it back down and home we go.
(Numbers = bolt order in a ten-bolt star pattern. Up to take off, down to put back.)

Sol's lock verses (ch19):
  Sun on your left and the Kettle below,
  dog to the notch where the old marks show,
  twice to the dark and once to the light,
  seat it and pin it and turn it tight.
  That is the way that you lock the door —
  count it back down and it's open once more.

Era 2 Keepers' invocation: first two lines only, as prayer. Sump skipping rhyme: whole thing, drifted: "old men show", "kiss it goodnight", "you're home once more"; "ten for the Comp'ny, ten and STOP" (jump).
Era 3 Count for the Dead: sung DESCENDING (ten to one), with lock verses reversed in order and turns inverted ("once to the dark and twice to the light", "pin it and seat it", "Kettle beside you and sun below", "that is the way that we close the door / count it back down; we are home once more"). It does not rhyme in the low numbers ("old true things never quite fit"). It is in fact the OPENING procedure — Idris taught it backward on purpose (ch20). Taboos from the First Tally: Never sing the Count on the Halo. Never let one Tally be the only one who knows it.

## Era 3 — The Dark, Year 419 (FIRST PERSON, Tally)
- Tally (born Mira), 19, of Hold Fourteen. Apprentice to Quill. Becomes the Tally.
- Quill, old Tally, blind; dies ch6. Lineage: "taught by Quill, taught by Sorrel, ... back to the First Tally, who would not give his name."
- Ness, 24, hullwalker. Tally's love. Hears the Halo "knocking."
- Aunt Veska, Hold boss. Trades Tally for water (ch9).
- Absalom Kine, Reeve of Brine. Rot-sick; Brine's ice failing; wants to open the Halo and own the door. Plans to kill Tally and Ness after. Dies at the window in first light, by choice, having lost the monopoly.
- Brother Cinder, Lamplighter; believes the Light returning brings back the Founders. Dies ch18.
- Marrow, the Reeve's lieutenant. Dies ch18.
- Lamplighters (cult: "the Light was stolen"; "the Quencher"). Tallies: "the Light went home". Hullwalkers: "the Halo died like a tired star."
- The knock: the Halo rings in a rhythm, stronger over years. Ten beats, pause... It is the Count sung UP (forward) — which Tally doesn't recognize because Tallies sing it down. Recognized ch21.
- Ending: Tally reads the plate, sings the full key over the Reeve's relay to every Hold and Brine, warns them to get behind the steel, counts it back down. First light. A voice from the throat sings "One for the hook—" and she answers "—and two for the line."
