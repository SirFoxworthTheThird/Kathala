# Outline — 21 chapters, strict braid A/B/C (Crossing / Account / Salvage)

Structural beats per era, synchronized by triad:
T1 (1–3) setup & hook | T2 (4–6) inciting / commitment | T3 (7–9) complication | T4 (10–12) MIDPOINT betrayal/reversal | T5 (13–15) escalation | T6 (16–18) crisis / dark moment | T7 (19–21) climaxes, in chronological order so nothing future spoils past.

Pacing clocks: Era 1 — days to the Fork (35 at ch4). Era 2 — days to Burning Day (12 at ch2). Era 3 — perigee passes left (7 at ch3).

---

## 1. What Is Written — A — CR 611 (Pell waypoint, day 0)
- S1: Coil housing accident during Wake rotation 31. Emeric Saal crushed. Annick attends with stylus and key; records death; speaks the transfer of his debt to wife Lise and two children asleep in the hold. Bonded watching; Dace among them. Annick counts breaths. The Book "listens"; Annick's voice.
- S2: Strongroom in the spine beside the Hold. Benn coughing. The cradle, the dead shelf (Voyages I–IV), the privy box, the brass Ordinances plaque, WHAT IS WRITTEN IS OWED, the drop lever. Benn makes her recite Ordinances (1, 4, 9, 11, 14, 20) as nightly habit; jokes no purser has used the drop in a hundred years. Annick plays back her own voice entry.
- S3: Courier "fold-runner" arrives. Annick logs it; packet for Master & Factor. In the Lantern, she watches Quarry and Lindqvist read; Szell's face. Dace finds Annick in the corridor: "You wrote his children into the Book." Their history.
- S4: Benn returns from officers' meeting grey. "Meridian is gone." "They'll want me to seal something I won't. When they come to you — remember the Ordinances. All of them." He gives her nothing more. She lies awake counting.

## 2. Liberty Owes Liberty — B — CR 929 (12 days to Burning Day)
- S1: Gallows Reach, Tamar. Hollis collects the Haskell portion; father's quota short; Hollis strikes 17-yr-old Ewan to the Deep Ice. Voice: counting, wry. The key on its chain. Catechism heard from schoolroom.
- S2: Called up: Ottilie Brask dead. Spoke-lift to the Hulk; the Crown; the spine; the Office; the motto. The Index stacks; her body; WE WERE CARGO in Charter script. Details wrong. Bastian. Marsh assigns: find the ballast killer before Burning Day & the Concordance verifier. Hall of the Account & Ash Case glimpsed. Catechism recited; Hollis's gloss on "Annick Vell".
- S3: Hollis alone with body at the morgue; finds index card in her shoe: "D-0447". Number tickles; doesn't place it. Wren calls — the 400 marks. End hook.

## 3. Perigee — C — CR 1317 (seven passes left)
- S1: Yara and Mitya dive the Hulk's outer ring in the cold window. Meltwater in free fall, House crests, drowned rooms. Salvage copper. Finds a Charter-band seal-reader in the ruins of an Office annex — brass, OFFICE OF THE ACCOUNT. The sealed Widow (Old Four) — CONDEMNED plate; two divers died there last year. "Fen's still in there, counting."
- S2: Back on Tamar, the Gutter. Gorse's cut. Pim's cough. News: Vey bailiff cutter Lawful Remedy in orbit, Paschal Recovery Company, the Account revived; Warden's broadcast. Ash Church chimes from the bell tower.
- S3: Sabeth Orrin waiting in her hab. Offers 50,000 for "Fen's cache" — "something Fen stole the night the Hulk fell." Seven passes. Yara says she'll think. She's already decided.

## 4. Article One — A — CR 611 (day 3–5; 35 days to the Fork)
- S1: Benn found dead in his bunk, concentrator hissing. Haddad uneasy. Annick takes the key from Benn's neck; by Art. 1 she is Purser when she lifts the Book. Speaks the entry of his death.
- S2: Summoned to the Lantern: Quarry, Lindqvist, Szell. The Receivers' order: Shale, assignment of debts, sale of the ship. Seal at the Fork in 35 days. Haddad's numbers on Shale (6–9 yrs). Lindqvist "It's done, Purser." Szell watching her; offers her a drink after.
- S3: Dace drags her into the Hold galleries to Tobiah Ruck and Marit Osei among cradles. They know. Tobiah asks her not to seal, and at the rising to seal him Master (Art. 14). She refuses; Marit's cool argument; Tobiah's gentleness. Annick: "I'll listen." Weight Song hummed.
- S4: Alone at the Book, she writes nothing. Shadow ledger of the dead. 

## 5. The Dead Accounts — B — CR 929 (10 days)
- S1: Hollis finds drawer D-0447 among dead accounts (extinct family). Ottilie's cipher notes — her teaching code. Decodes fragments: "The Account was not copied. It was composed." "The Account is silent." "Ask the rings." The dead shelf in the vault: Voyages One, Two, Four. "Three lost before Landfall."
- S2: Marsh's gentle warning over tea, in his zero-g office with a bonsai in a sphere. Assigns Hollis as liaison to the verifier. Thorne arrives on Probity: artificial eye, "Paper is paper."
- S3: Down to Saltmarch underbelly. Calla Dray. Jory. She says Ottilie was coming to meet her that night with "something that would end the Account." Calla spits at him; gives him one thing anyway.
- S4: Someone has been through his quarters. Wren calls: an Office man came asking about her portion.

## 6. The Ash Case — C — CR 1317 (six passes)
- S1: Ash Church; Mother Quell; the charred Ash Case under glass; the Church's story of the Burning; "the Purser who would have sold us"; ash on wrists; Fen songs ("Fen went down with a lamp in his hand"). Quell's diver past. Weight Song, divers' version.
- S2: Mitya in. They plan; Sabeth's partial map of the spine.
- S3: Dive: into the spine via the Old Hold — cradle racks underwater like drowned pews. Office upper levels; ruined Index stacks. Collapse; Mitya saves her. Six passes.
- S4: Surfacing, they see lights — another crew in the Hulk. Gorse's. The Company's bailiffs are posting writs in the Gutter.

## 7. Air Debt — A — CR 611 (~day 14; 21 days to Fork)
- S1: Annick's spying: proctor rotations, lattice locks of the Lantern. Kolar's suspicion. The air meters; Wake life; a bonded woman faints from rationing her own air.
- S2: Tobiah shows her the Weight Song; "down the sea"; she explains Art. 9 spacer slang; he laughs: "So the ship's ours when the Book's in the ballast." Warmth. His past (Callis quarries). Szell mentioned obliquely as "a friend on the bridge".
- S3: Haddad: Benn's concentrator — he kept it; "a filter that shouldn't fail like that." Not proof.
- S4: Quarry over dinner, testing her; the Fork moved up — coil cooling faster: 17 days. End: alarm — proctors find weapons in Tank 2's access crawlway; Dace and Joss arrested.

## 8. The Sale — B — CR 929 (9 days)
- S1: Decoding more: Ottilie indexed Marsh's draft conveyance to the Ostrava Labor Trust. The Sale. Lots list: Gallows Reach first; Wren's household in Saltmarch on lot nine.
- S2: Confronts Thorne at Probity's berth: "I verify title. What title is used for is not my office." He tells Hollis the signing is set for Burning Day.
- S3: Attempt on his life — spoke-lift shaft; he kills a House Guard man. Gritty.
- S4: Tries to pay Wren's 400 — Clearances frozen pending "a review". He understands.

## 9. First Satisfaction — C — CR 1317 (five passes)
- S1: Assembly hall: Factor Kade presents the writ; Advocate Dunn objects; Clerk-Notary Rhee: only a "certified contrary record" voids it. Warden Asch's collaboration.
- S2: Registration: Pim and Yara DNA-tested; the Tull portion compounded into an absurd figure; Pim marked "suitable for satisfaction pending medical".
- S3: Dive: Gorse's crew; tether cut; fight in the drowned cargo hall (the old Hall of the Account); Yara wounds a man. They find the route through to the strongroom level.
- S4: On the dock, Yara sees Sabeth step out of a Company shuttle. Hook.

## 10. The Price of a Name — A — CR 611 (~day 22) — MIDPOINT
- S1: Dace and Joss in the proctors' brig. Kolar beats Joss. Quarry's offer: a name for Dace. Otherwise forfeiture, spacing.
- S2: Annick's night of calculation (ledger of lives). She gives Marit Osei.
- S3: Marit spaced from the forward lock before the Wake rotation. Marit's last look at Annick; her words. Annick records the death and transfer of her debt to grandchildren in the hold — voice shaking.
- S4: Dace released; Quarry: "Watch the First Officer, Purser." Tobiah's look; he says nothing. Dace suspects.

## 11. Rings in the Lattice — B — CR 929 (6 days) — MIDPOINT
- S1: In the Account master vault with Thorne's verification visit. Thorne's eye reads the founding page: Council seal not purser's; rings ~9 years after Landfall; silent. Forgery.
- S2: Thorne: "Interesting. Not sufficient." Needs sworn lodging by an uncompromised officer; or a sealed contrary record. Hollis copies the reading onto a lattice slice.
- S3: To Marsh. Trap: guards; Hollis charged with Ottilie's murder; apartment burned; Wren in "protective custody" in the Crown. He escapes down the spine shaft into the condemned sections near the old cisterns.

## 12. The Client — C — CR 1317 (four passes) — MIDPOINT
- S1: Yara follows Sabeth; overhears her with Kade: the cache must come to them unopened — destroyed. "The Speaker's family always believed Fen stole proof against the Account."
- S2: Confrontation with Mitya: he's been paid by Sabeth separately. Gorse owns him. Fight; love; shame.
- S3: Advocate Dunn finds Yara: "If you find it, bring it to me." Three buyers.
- S4: Night: the Crown ring tears away at perigee; burning streak over Tamar; four passes. Yara decides: she dives for herself. Mitya begs to come.

## 13. The Master's Authority — A — CR 611 (~day 33)
- S1: Lindqvist calls Annick to his day room: he won't take them to Shale; asks her to record his authority free of the Factor. She can't without Quarry's countersign.
- S2: Szell approaches: she's with the rising; plan: at the Fork she takes the Lantern; Annick seals Szell Master.
- S3: Haddad: the concentrator was tampered — maintenance code erased. Murder.
- S4: Tobiah confirms Szell; "Seal Szell. She can fly." Dace won't speak to Annick. The coil spins up for the jump to the Fork.

## 14. We Were Cargo — B — CR 929 (3 days)
- S1: Hollis in the Hold's warrens (Saltmarch / Hulk underdecks). Calla. They need each other.
- S2: Hold watcher saw Bastian visit Ottilie the night she died.
- S3: Night talk: the ice key; his mother; Gallows Reach; Calla's spoon. Calla's plan: rise on Burning Day; she wants the proof broadcast. Hollis wants Thorne. Compromise.
- S4: Concordance squadron arrives — transports like empty hulls waiting.

## 15. What Is Writ — C — CR 1317 (three passes)
- S1: Dive with Mitya, fragile trust. Through the Old Hold to the strongroom door: WHAT IS WRIT— — OWED. Begin cutting.
- S2: Gorse's crew attacks; hull shudder; Mitya pinned; he makes her leave. His death.
- S3: Tamar: first satisfaction shipments begin. Pim taken by Company — collateral. Three passes.

## 16. The Fork — A — CR 611 (day 36) — CRISIS
- S1: Jump to the Fork. The Rising. Hold thaws 400 sleepers; some die. Proctors fight. Joss killed. Dace wounded.
- S2: Annick in the strongroom; proctors try the door; Szell's signal; she seals Szell Master; Lantern falls; Lindqvist dead "by mutineers"; Quarry captured.
- S3: Annick carries the Book toward the Lantern; outside the captain's day room overhears Szell, Tobiah, Quarry: the compact; burn the Book publicly; raise the Liberty Account. Szell about Benn: "I made sure he'd never be asked." Tobiah knew about Marit.
- S4: She walks in. They greet her as a hero. She says nothing. The ship turns for Absalom.

## 17. Burning Day — B — CR 929 — CRISIS
- S1: Hollis infiltrates the Hulk in grey; corners Bastian; recorded confession; Marsh ordered it.
- S2: Marsh in his office; the bonsai; the truth: Houses always knew; "Every ledger is a forgery"; Ottilie found purser's key 0447 in old manifests, recognized it on Hollis; the purser's box unopened 300 years. Hollis looks at the worn stamp. Guards.
- S3: Hall of the Account. The Mercy. Ash Case. Thorne. Wren in the gallery among House wards. The Hold rises early; gunfire; lights go out.

## 18. The Empty Box — C — CR 1317 (two passes) — CRISIS
- S1: Kade's offer aboard Lawful Remedy; Pim in the medbay; velvet threat. Bring the cache unopened.
- S2: Dunn and Quell plead. Yara dives alone.
- S3: Finishes Mitya's cut. Strongroom: empty cradle; dead shelf I, II, IV; brass plaque crusted (she photographs for salvage); privy box open and empty; a body in grey with a chain of office. She assumes it's Fen. Nothing. Takes dead book IV.
- S4: Surfaces. Two passes; one cold window left. Despair.

## 19. Down the Sea — A — CR 611 (~day 58, Landfall) — CLIMAX
- S1: Arrival at Absalom; Tamar's ice. Founders prepare the Burning. Szell: seal the Liberty Account after.
- S2: Dace in infirmary; she knows about Marit; reckoning; love.
- S3: Night: Annick's final entry (full text). Reads it aloud in the Hold among sleepers. Commits Book to the ballast — the drop. Dead book III into the cradle. Lead plate in privy box. Key to Dace — wound dressing.
- S4: The Burning in the cargo bay; thermite; crowd; Tobiah's speech. Afterward: "There's no key." Search. Denunciation. Tobiah's mercy: the ice. Dace goes with her. Shuttle down. The Book in the dark water.

## 20. The Fourth Cistern — B — CR 929 — CLIMAX
- S1: Dark; Calla's people free Hollis; recorded confession to Thorne; Hollis lodges under oath; Thorne declines verification. Sale collapses.
- S2: Severine orders the Mercy Guard; frigate fires; Hulk hit. Hollis gets Wren to Calla's shuttle.
- S3: Strongroom. Marsh. Gunfight; Hollis shot in side; Marsh dead. Key opens privy box: lead plate ART. 9 — A.V. Plaque. The drop. Fourth Cistern; smoke hood; dark water; the Book.
- S4: Breach; bulkheads; freezing chamber. Opens Book. Annick's voice. Learns he's Dace's line. Calla on handset: Wren safe; Severine fled with a copy. He makes his entry. Dies.

## 21. Before Witnesses — C — CR 1317 (last pass) — CLIMAX
- S1: In the sled, the plaque photo enhanced: Art. 9; Art. 11; Art. 1. The ballast. The Widow.
- S2: Dives the Widow; finds Hollis; tally H. FEN; the Book; key in the hasp. Realizes the strongroom body was someone else.
- S3: Exchange: dead book IV for Pim via Dunn. Pim safe.
- S4: Reads under seal: seal-reader → sled → Ash Church bell tower → everyone and Rhee. Annick's voice; Hollis's voice. Her entry. Rhee voids the writ.
- S5: Leaves the Book with Hollis, keeps the key. Escape; the Hulk burns into Absalom.
- S6: Coda in the Ash Church: Ash Case inner plate VOY. III. The key. Last line.
