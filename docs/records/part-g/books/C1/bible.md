# THE MERCIFUL INCREASE — series bible

## Core idea
A debt ledger (the Book) binds a colony across 700 years. Three Pursers — people who hold the Key and the Book together (Article 1) — in three eras. Each era vilifies the previous era's hero. The Book is hidden (era 1), found and recorded in too late (era 2), and read aloud to everyone, awake (era 3). Then it burns for real.

Theme: whoever holds the record holds the people. A hidden record is a weapon; a record read to everyone is freedom. "What is written is owed."

## Calendar
Charter Reckoning (CR) persists as the legal calendar for 700 years (the creditor's calendar outlives everyone).
- Era 1: THE CROSSING · CR 611 (Landfall at Absalom happens at end of this year)
- Era 2: THE ACCOUNT · CR 929 (318 years after Landfall)
- Era 3: THE SALVAGE · CR 1317 (706 years after Landfall; 388 after era 2)

## Voices
- Era 1: close third, past tense, Annick Vell. Precise, cold, ledger-minded, ship detail.
- Era 2: first person, past tense, Hollis Fen. Noir, wry, bitter, counts things.
- Era 3: close third, PRESENT tense, Yara Tull. Physical, sensory, immediate.

## The Purser's Ordinances (Charter law, brass plaque in the strongroom)
- Art. 1 — The Purser is whosoever holds the Key and the Book together.
- Art. 4 — What is written under seal is owed. (painted over strongroom door: WHAT IS WRITTEN IS OWED)
- Art. 9 — Should the vessel be taken by any party not lawfully in command, the Purser shall commit the Book to the ballast, that it be not suborned.
- Art. 11 — An entry read aloud under seal before witnesses stands as the Book itself.
- Art. 14 — The Master's authority is that which the Book records. (ship systems obey the recorded Master)
- Art. 20 — A debt discharged under seal is discharged forever, against all heirs and assigns.

## Anchors
1. THE BOOK — iron case ~40x30x12 cm, 18 kg, Charter sunburst-and-scales on cover. Lattice core; entries are spoken and inscribed; "the Book listens" — keeps the purser's voice. Page-glass shows entries when key is in hasp. Cold-cell battery.
2. DEAD BOOKS — the retired Books of Voyages I–IV on the "dead shelf." Annick burns VOYAGE III as decoy. The Ash Case (charred) has inner plate "M.I. VOY. III". Era 2 and era 3 dead shelf: I, II, IV only ("Three was lost before Landfall").
3. THE KEY — purser's key No. 0447: iron, palm-length, notched bit, lattice bead in the bow. Annick → Dace (hidden in wound dressing) → ice families → Hollis's mother → Hollis (wears it on a chain, "the ice key") → in the Book's hasp in Hollis's hand → Yara.
4. THE SHIP — Merciful Increase (era 1) → the Hulk, core of Increase Station (era 2) → the falling Hulk, drowned decks (era 3).
5. TANK 4 / the Fourth Cistern / "Old Four" / "the Widow". Strongroom has "the drop" — lead-lined chute to Tank 4 (Art. 9 mechanism).
6. THE WEIGHT SONG — bonded work song; words drift.
   Era 1: "Heave the iron, heave it slow, / the Book is heavy, down we go. / What is written, that is owed, / and the dark's a long cold road. / Heave and heave and we'll be free / when the Book goes down the sea."
   Era 2 (Houses' version): "...we'll be free / when the Book is ash, says he." Ice miners: "...when the ash is paid, says he."
   Era 3 divers: mixture, half-nonsense.
7. WRIST NUMBERS — bonded tattoo inside left wrist (V5-2231 etc). Era 2: ash marked on wrist on Burning Day. Era 3: Ash Church ash on wrist.
8. Strongroom door motto. Era 3: flaked to "WHAT IS WRIT— — OWED".

## Geography
- Absalom: gas giant, ochre/rust bands. Moon TAMAR: ice moon; under-ice towns.
- Merciful Increase: 1,100 m Charter transport (CR 560 build). Bow dome = "the Lantern" (bridge). SPIN RING (crew quarters, mess, officers). SPINE (zero-g): the HOLD (cold-sleep cradles, 3,612 bonded), the purser's deck / STRONGROOM (beside the Hold), BALLAST TANKS 1–4 around the spine (water for reaction mass & shielding; Tank 4 forwardmost, adjacent to strongroom). CARGO BAY (later the Hall of the Account). FOLD COIL aft.
- Era 2 Increase Station: the Hulk + "the Crown" (added spin ring where Houses live) + docks + spoke lifts. Office of the Account in the old spine (zero-g, handholds). Hall of the Account = old cargo bay, Ash Case displayed on the spot of the Burning. Old Hold = memorial gallery of empty cradle racks. Fourth Cistern sealed 200 yrs ago after "a fever in the water", CONDEMNED plates.
- Tamar era 2: Saltmarch (capital, under the ice shelf), Gallows Reach (oldest settlement, founded by the first Struck exiles), the Deep Ice (penal mines).
- Era 3: Tamar's divers' town "the Gutter" in Ringfall dock; the Ash Church in Saltmarch with a "bell tower" — salvaged Charter-band array that rings broadcast chimes.

## ERA 1 — THE CROSSING — CR 611
Voyage V of the Merciful Increase, Callis → Absalom via the Long Lane, 11 fold jumps. Courier catches them at Pell waypoint (after jump 8). The Fork (jump 9 waypoint): branch to Shale (one jump) or Absalom (two more).
Meridian Charter Company has failed; its paper bought by the Receivers of the Bank of Vey. Orders: divert to Shale, assign the Book's debts to the Shale Concern (2.1 g mining world, 6–9 yr life expectancy), sell the ship.
Bonded wear dun coveralls with Book number; air meters on wrist ("breathing is billable" — air debt). Wake rotations of ~240 bonded awake for 3 months.
Cast:
- ANNICK VELL, 34, assistant purser → Purser. Born bonded on Callis; mother died in a Charter mine; at 17 took the Charter's clerk service bond. Bonded call her "the Ink". Counts breaths. Keeps a shadow ledger of the dead in her head.
- DACE VELL, 22, half-sister; Annick raised her; bonded (Annick couldn't pay her out). Hull tech on the Wake. Rebel. Lover of Joss.
- AURELIO BENN, 71, Purser; bad lungs; taught Annick. Refuses to seal the assignment; murdered (oxygen concentrator tampered) by NADIA SZELL.
- CAPTAIN HARROW LINDQVIST, 60. Weary, decent to crew. Wavers. Shot at the Fork by Szell's man (Lt. Orme), blamed on mutineers.
- FIRST OFFICER NADIA SZELL, 45. Brilliant pilot, cold humour. Wants Absalom's patent for herself. Allies with Tobiah. Becomes Master. Founder (House Constance's secret foremother).
- FACTOR EDWIN QUARRY, 50. Charter/Receivers' legal agent. Elegant, vicious. Captured at the Fork; co-opted by Szell; designs the Liberty Debt.
- TOBIAH RUCK, 40. Bonded ex-quarryman, huge, scarred, gentle voice. Leads the Rising. Makes the deal ("somebody's got to pay for the air"). Spares Annick: exile.
- MARIT OSEI, 56. Bonded, ex-union advocate, strategist. Annick gives her name to save Dace; spaced.
- DR YUSUF HADDAD, 55. Cryo-master & doctor. Annick's friend. Keeps Benn's concentrator; proves murder.
- JOSS IMRE "Cutter", 19, rebel, Dace's lover. Killed at the Fork.
- PROCTOR-SERGEANT VASHTI KOLAR, head of Quarry's 24 proctors (grey armour, stingers, flechette carbines).
- LT. CASPAR ORME — Szell's man.
- EMERIC SAAL — bonded, crushed in coil housing, ch1; wife Lise and children in the hold.
Annick's final entry: records the Shale order, Benn's murder by Szell, Lindqvist shot by Orme, the Founders' compact (Szell, Ruck, Quarry), her own betrayal of Marit; discharges every debt under Art. 20; vests the patent of Absalom in the people of the hold and their heirs in common; commits the Book to the ballast under Art. 9; "I have put the dead book of the third voyage in the cradle." "What is written is owed. I have written what we owe each other." "Read under seal before witnesses, who were asleep."
She leaves in the privy box a lead plate scratched: ART. 9 — A.V. Gives key to Dace. Refuses to seal the Liberty Account: "There's no key." Exiled with Dace to Tamar's ice (first Struck → Gallows Reach).

## ERA 2 — THE ACCOUNT — CR 929
Population ~1.9 million. Seven Houses (virtue names, Founders unnamed by pious rule): Constance, Patience, Temperance, Prudence, Fortitude, Clemency, Providence. Speaker: LADY SEVERINE CONSTANCE.
Liberty Debt: every ballast child inherits a "portion"; paid in labour marks; "Clear" when paid; defaulters "Struck" to the Deep Ice. Assessors: grey long coats, brass scales pin, a tally, sidearm.
Burning Day: annual, the Speaker reads the Mercy, people mark ash on their wrists; Ash Case displayed in the Hall.
Catechism of Mercy: Who carried us? The Increase carried us. / Who would have sold us? The Purser would have sold us. / What was her name? Annick Vell, and it is written in ash. / Who saved us? The Founders saved us. / What did they burn? They burned the Book. / What do we owe? We owe our Liberty. / To whom? To those who paid for it.
"Purser's blood" = slur.
Concordance of Worlds: Bureau of Titles. Ostrava Labor Trust buys labour. Squadron: 3 labour transports + 2 escorts; survey cruiser Probity. House fleet: the Mercy Guard; frigate Constance's Hand.
The Sale: Houses sell the Account (all un-Clear portions) to Ostrava; ballast shipped as contract labour; Houses get Concordance peerage. Needs Concordance verification of title (Thorne).
The forgery: the Account's founding page claims to be a true copy of the Book's last state sealed by the Purser at Landfall. It carries a Founders' Council seal, not a purser's lattice signature; its lattice "rings" date it ~9 years after Landfall; and it is SILENT (no voice — every Charter book keeps the purser's voice).
Cast:
- HOLLIS FEN, 41, Assessor of the Second Rank. Born in Gallows Reach, ice family. Lifted at 12 by Marsh. Divorced from IMKE (refinery chemist, Saltmarch); daughter WREN, 15, 400 marks short of Clear. Counts things. Wears the ice key (0447, worn stamp) — from his mother.
- OTTILIE BRASK, 63, Keeper of the Index. Taught Hollis lattice reading ("read it like rings in a tree"). Killed (neck broken) in the Index stacks; "WE WERE CARGO" painted in Charter script (Hold uses ice-script). Hid notes in dead-account drawer D-0447 (his key number).
- LUCAN MARSH, 68, Chief Assessor, House Prudence. Father figure. Ordered the murder. "Every ledger is a forgery. The question is who holds the pen." Killed by Hollis in the strongroom (his body is found in era 3 and mistaken for Fen).
- BASTIAN ODUYA, 24, junior assessor, House-client. Killer. Confesses (recorded) ch17.
- ISAAK THORNE, Verifier-Commander, Concordance Bureau of Titles. 50s. Artificial left eye reads lattice. "Paper is paper." Refuses verification in ch20.
- CALLA DRAY, 34, Hold leader. Ex-Deep Ice miner, lost two fingers to frost. Hollis struck her brother JORY (died in the Deep Ice).
- EWAN HASKELL, 17 — boy Hollis strikes in ch2.
Era 2 end: Hollis lodges forgery under oath (cleared by Bastian's confession); Thorne declines; Sale collapses; transports withdraw; Severine flees on her courier yacht with a COPY of the Account; frigate fires on the Hulk; Wren gets out with Calla; Hollis kills Marsh in the strongroom (shot in the side himself), opens privy box with key → lead plate ART. 9 — A.V. → the drop → Fourth Cistern (dives in with a smoke hood) → Book. Reads Annick. Breach; bulkheads seal; trapped in the cistern access chamber, freezing. Writes his entry: forgery, Ottilie, Marsh, Bastian, the Sale, Severine's copy ("let any court that sees it know it for a forgery"), his own sins (Ewan Haskell), "Annick Vell was not a traitor." Learns from the Book he is Dace's line. Dies. Water later floods the chamber and freezes him.

## ERA 3 — THE SALVAGE — CR 1317
Population ~400,000 after the Long Dimming (lane closed ~200 years). Warden of Tamar CORIN ASCH + weak Common Assembly. Lane reopened ~40 yrs ago.
The Hulk: station-keeping died ~30 yrs ago; elliptical orbit round Absalom, perigee dips into upper atmosphere every ~3.5 days; heating thaws the frozen interior; divers dive the meltwater in the "cold window" near apogee. Count of passes left = chapter clock (ch3: seven; ch6 six; ch9 five; ch12 four; ch15 three; ch18 two; ch21 last).
Ash Church: venerates the Burning; the Ash Case relic; Mother QUELL. Slang: "to vell" = to cheat a count; "ballast" = poor; "struck" = broke. "Fen the Turncoat" songs; "Fen's still in there, counting"; "Fen's cache" — something Fen stole from the Office the night the Hulk fell.
Paschal Recovery Company of Vey: bailiff cutter Lawful Remedy; FACTOR AUREL KADE; Vey court CLERK-NOTARY ANSEL RHEE (needed to certify each "satisfaction"; voids writ only on a "certified contrary record"). The Constance copy of the Account bought at a Vey estate auction; compounded portions; "first satisfaction" = 40,000 bodies.
Cast:
- YARA TULL, 29, hulk-diver. Mother died diving. Brother PIM, 14, rime-lung. Owes GORSE (salvage boss).
- DMITRI "MITYA" SAAR, 31, diving partner, ex-lover, gambler, owes Gorse. Paid by Sabeth secretly (ch12). Dies saving Yara (ch15).
- SABETH ORRIN — client; Company agent.
- ADVOCATE MIREILLE DUNN — Assembly.
- MOTHER QUELL — Ash priest, ex-diver, 70s.
- Yara's tools: hardsuit ("shell"), sled, cutter, the Charter-band seal-reader she salvages in ch3 (from Office of the Account).
Era 3 end: realises Art. 9 from plaque photo; dives the Widow; finds Hollis (grey coat, pin, tally stamped H. FEN), the Book, the key in the hasp. Holds Key and Book: Purser. Exchanges dead book VOYAGE IV (taken ch18) to Kade for Pim. Reads under seal via seal-reader → sled → Church bell tower array → whole system + Rhee. Plays Annick's and Hollis's voices. Rhee voids writ (Art. 20, Art. 11). Yara's entry: "Read under seal before witnesses, who were awake." Leaves Book with Hollis, keeps the key. Hulk burns into Absalom: the true Burning. Coda: Ash Case inner plate VOY. III.

## NO-SPOILER RULES
- Era 2 & 3 believe the Book burned. No hint of survival before ch19.
- Era 2's history villainises Annick ("the Purser would have sold us"). Does not name Founders. Does not say who won in what way beyond "Founders saved us".
- Era 3 must not mention Wren, Marsh, the Sale/Ostrava, the forgery before ch11, how Fen ended. Fen legend = turncoat; cache = "something he stole". Strongroom body (Marsh) mistaken for Fen in ch18 (red herring).
- Era 3's Ash Case inner plate revealed only in ch21.
- Fourth Cistern / Widow / Tank 4: mentioned innocently everywhere; significance only from ch19.
