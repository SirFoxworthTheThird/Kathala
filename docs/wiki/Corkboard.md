# Corkboard

The **Corkboard** is an index-card view of your whole story — the classic way to see structure at a glance and shuffle it.

Each **chapter is a column**; each **scene (event) is a card** showing its title, synopsis, POV character, and status.

---

## Scene status

Every card carries a status pill, and you can change it right on the card:

**Idea → Outline → Draft → Revised → Final**

The same statuses drive the Scene Status panel on the [Dashboard](Dashboard) and the status colouring in the [Character Arc grid](Character-Arc).

---

## Restructuring

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Drag a card** within a column | Reorders scenes inside that chapter |
| **Drag a card** to another column | Moves the scene to that chapter |
| **Click a card's title** | Jumps to that scene in the chapter detail, with the time cursor set to it |

The timeline order updates to match. It is the same set of events as the [Timeline](Timeline), shown as a board — reorder here or there and both stay in sync.

---

## Undo

Reordering is a single undoable action: **reordering two events puts both back, never half the swap**. See [Undo, Redo & Recent Changes](Undo-and-Redo).

---

## Not available while reading

The Corkboard is a writing screen, so [reading mode](Reading-Mode) removes it — along with its address, so typing the URL in returns you to the dashboard.
