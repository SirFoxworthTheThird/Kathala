# World Dashboard

Opening a world lands you on its dashboard — a bird's-eye view of the whole project.

---

## Stat tiles

Tiles summarise the timeline, cast, maps, relationships, items, snapshot coverage, and continuity status. **The tiles are links** — click **Timeline**, **Characters**, **Maps**, or any other to jump straight to that area.

Worlds with linked timelines also show a **Timeline Links** summary.

Below the tiles sit recent events, scene status, writing progress, and the analytics panels: [Cast Balance](Cast-Balance), [Plot Threads](Plot-Threads), and [Motifs & Themes](Motifs).

> In [reading mode](Reading-Mode) the dashboard becomes a way in rather than a progress report — the panels that measure the manuscript rather than the story are hidden.

---

## Getting around

Every screen in a world shares a **left navigation rail**. By default it is a slim strip of icons, to keep your workspace wide.

- **Hover** it to slide out the full labels.
- Click the **pin** at the bottom to keep it expanded.
- The everyday screens (Dashboard, Timeline, Manuscript, Characters, Maps) sit above a **More** divider, with the rest below.

On a phone the rail is replaced by a **☰ menu** in the top bar. The top bar itself keeps the world name, the [time cursor](Core-Concepts), search (**Ctrl/⌘+K**), and the Writer's Brief, Continuity, and Help tools.

---

## Writing Progress

As you write scene prose in the [Manuscript](Manuscript) view, PlotWeave keeps a lightweight per-day log of the words you add or cut. The **Writing Progress** panel turns that into an at-a-glance readout:

| Readout | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total words** | Across the whole manuscript |
| **Words today** | The net change since midnight; green when you've added |
| **Day streak** | Consecutive days written. A blank day *today* doesn't break a run you're still in the middle of |
| **Daily session goal** | Set a per-day word goal; a ring fills as you hit it |
| **Burndown bar** | Progress against your book **word target**, with percent complete and words to go |
| **14-day strip** | Daily output, so you can see recent momentum |

Set the word target under *Manuscript* in [World Settings](World-Settings).

---

## Deadline & projection

Give the book a **deadline** (also under *Manuscript* in World Settings) and the panel adds a forecast:

- The **words/day** you'd need to finish on time.
- A **projected finish date**, from your recent pace.
- An **on track** / **behind pace** badge.

It's an honest read on whether your current rhythm will get you there.

---

## Travelling with the world

The writing log, word target, and deadline all travel through [export/import](Export-and-Import), so your streak and history survive a backup or a move to another device.
