# Calendar & Character Ages

By default PlotWeave measures story time in **in-world days** — day 0 is the start of a timeline, and each event's *travel days* push the clock forward.

Turn those day numbers into real dates by giving your world a **calendar**.

---

## Enabling a calendar

In [World Settings](World-Settings), click **Enable calendar** to start from a standard 12-month, 365-day year, then tailor it:

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Start year** | The year that in-world day 0 falls in |
| **Year suffix** | An era label shown after the year — *AC*, *TA*, *AR* |
| **Months** | Rename them, set each month's length in days, add or remove months |

A fantasy calendar can have any number of months of any length.

With a calendar set, in-world dates appear wherever the day clock is shown — most visibly the active event's date in the [Writer's Brief](Writers-Brief).

---

## Character ages

Give a character an optional **birth date** on the **Overview** tab of their profile. The month/day/year pickers use your calendar's months.

PlotWeave then computes and shows the character's **age at the event under the time cursor** in the Writer's Brief — counting birthdays passed, so it stays correct even with irregular month lengths.

A character born *after* the current moment simply shows no age.

---

## Calendar view

Once a calendar is set, the **Calendar** view appears in the navigation rail. It lays your events onto month grids by their in-world date, so you can see the shape of your story in time.

- Each month your story touches gets a grid.
- Events appear as **chips on their day**.
- **Flashbacks** are marked with a small clock icon.

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Click** an event chip | Jump to it in the timeline |
| **Drag** a chip to another day | **Pin** its in-world date |

Pinning sets the event's explicit in-world time, **overriding the travel-day clock** for that event. Use it for flashbacks and flash-forwards, or to nail a scene to a specific date.

---

## Multiple timelines

Each timeline's clock starts at day 0 by default. For multi-era stories, give a timeline a **start day** under *Timelines* in [World Settings](World-Settings) so chronological merges and the calendar place both eras where they actually fall.

An event's **pinned** in-world day stays relative to its own timeline's clock.

---

## Travelling with the world

The calendar and every birth date travel through [export/import](Export-and-Import), so shared or backed-up worlds keep their dates intact.
