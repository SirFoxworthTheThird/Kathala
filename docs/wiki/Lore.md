# Lore

Lore is your world's **reference wiki** — history, rules, and mythology that don't change with time.

---

## Organising

Pages live in **categories** (Artefacts, Peoples, Places…) and carry tags. Create a category, then add pages inside it.

---

## Writing a page

Each page has a **Markdown editor** with an **Edit / Preview** toggle.

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The article heading |
| **Body** | Markdown |
| **Tags** | Freeform labels |
| **Linked entities** | Characters, items, or locations this page is about |
| **Revealed at** | The first event at which the page becomes visible |

---

## Linking entities

**Link entities** associates a page with characters, items, or locations. The page then appears:

- In those entities' **Lore** sections, on their own detail pages.
- In the **[Writer's Brief](Writers-Brief)**, when one of them is present at the current moment.

It's a two-way link — you never have to remember which side you made it from.

---

## Reveal events

**Revealed at** chooses the first event at which a page becomes visible.

- A page with no reveal event is always available.
- A page with one appears from that event onward, and is marked **NEW** in the Writer's Brief at exactly that event.

Turn on the **revealed-only filter** in the Lore index to hide future knowledge at the current cursor — the writer's version of not spoiling yourself.

In [reading mode](Reading-Mode) this is enforced rather than optional: a lore page waits until the reader has met what it is about.

---

## Generating lore with AI

**Generate with AI** builds out your wiki in bulk. Pages are **filed into categories by name**, and categories are created automatically. A page with a matching title is updated in place rather than duplicated.

See [Generating with AI](AI-Generation).

---

## Sequels

A [sequel](Sequels) can turn book one's chapters into a recap — one lore page per chapter, grouped in a *Previously — {book one}* category — and carry your world-building lore forward.

---

## Common problems

**A page isn't in the Writer's Brief.**
It needs either a link to a character present at the active event, or a reveal event at or before it.

**Markdown isn't rendering.**
Toggle **Preview**. The editor itself shows raw Markdown.
