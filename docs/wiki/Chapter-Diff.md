# Chapter Diff

**Compare chapters** shows exactly what changed between any two points in the story — who moved, who gained or lost items, who died, and how relationships shifted.

Open it from the **Compare** control on the chapter bar, available whenever a time cursor is set.

---

## Choosing what to compare

The diff opens comparing the active moment against the preceding one. Use the picker at the top to compare against **any other point** in the world; the diff updates immediately.

---

## What it reports

### Character movements

Every character whose location changed, with the before and after.

### Relationship changes

Every relationship whose label, strength, sentiment, or active state shifted.

### Item relocations

Every item that moved between locations or inventories.

Each section marks entries as **added**, **removed**, **changed**, or unchanged, so a large diff can be skimmed for the parts that matter.

---

## Navigating from a finding

Click a row to jump to the relevant entity with the time cursor set to the compared moment.

---

## Common problems

**The diff shows nothing.**
No snapshots differ between those two moments — all state is being [inherited](Core-Concepts). Compare points further apart.

**A character I know moved isn't listed.**
Confirm a snapshot recorded the new location at that event. Updating only the movement waypoints without changing the snapshot location leaves the recorded position unchanged.
