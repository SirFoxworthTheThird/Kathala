# Structure Board

The **Structure** board checks your story against a **beat sheet**.

Pick a template and each of its beats appears as a slot, in order and tinted by act.

---

## Templates

| Template | |
|---|---|
| **Three-Act** | The classic setup / confrontation / resolution shape |
| **Save the Cat** | Blake Snyder's fifteen beats |
| **Hero's Journey** | The monomyth stages |

**Switching templates keeps your tags.** A scene tagged with a Three-Act beat simply won't fill a Save-the-Cat slot until you assign it there, so you can commit to one framework at a time without losing work.

---

## Filling the board

- **Assign a scene** to a beat from its *"+ Assign a scene…"* picker. The slot then shows that scene — click it to jump to the event in the timeline.
- You can also set a scene's beat from its **card on the Timeline**.

---

## What the board tells you

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **X / N beats placed** | How much of the structure is filled — so gaps (a missing midpoint, no clear climax) stand out |
| **Out of order** | A beat whose scene falls *earlier* in the story than a later beat's scene — catching a structure that's been shuffled |

---

## Not available while reading

Structure is a writing screen, so [reading mode](Reading-Mode) removes it and closes its address.
