# Getting Started

This page walks through building a world from nothing. If you already have a draft, a synopsis, or just want to look around first, you have faster options — see [Before you start by hand](#before-you-start-by-hand) at the bottom.

---

## Prerequisites

PlotWeave is open and you can see the **world selector**. See [Installation](Installation) if not.

---

## Step 1: Create a world

A **world** is the top-level container for one story or universe.

1. Click **New World** on the world selector.
2. Enter a name, and optionally a description.
3. Click **Create World**.

The world opens immediately, and PlotWeave offers a **four-step setup guide**: create the first timeline and event, add a main character, place that character at the opening moment, then continue to the Timeline. Every optional step has **Skip**, so you can leave the guide and build in any order.

---

## Step 2: Create a timeline

A **timeline** is an ordered sequence of chapters. Every world needs at least one; most only ever need one.

1. Go to **Timeline** in the left navigation rail.
2. Click **Create Timeline** (or **New Timeline** if one already exists).
3. Give it a name — "Main Story" is fine.

---

## Step 3: Add a chapter

1. In the timeline view, click **Add Chapter**.
2. Enter a title. The number is filled in for you.
3. Optionally add a synopsis.

Chapters inherit all snapshots from the immediately preceding chapter in the same timeline, so a chapter starts where the last one ended.

---

## Step 4: Add an event

An **event** is a scene or beat, and the unit all state is recorded against.

1. Open the chapter's detail page (the **open** button on the chapter row).
2. Click **Add Event** and give it a title.
3. Expand the event card to fill in the rest: description, status (Idea → Outline → Draft → Revised → Final), POV character, location, characters and items involved, tags, and travel days.

The event now appears on the chapter bar at the bottom of the screen — that bar is your [time cursor](Core-Concepts).

---

## Step 5: Add a character

1. Go to **Characters**.
2. Click **Add Character** and enter a name.
3. Open the character to fill in the biography, aliases, portrait, colour, and an optional birth date.

Building a whole cast by hand is slow — **Generate with AI** next to *Add Character* does it in bulk. See [Generating with AI](AI-Generation).

---

## Step 6: Record the character's state at that event

1. Move the time cursor to your first event (bottom bar).
2. Open the character → **Current State** tab.
3. Set their location, alive status, inventory, travel mode, and any status note.

Changes save as you make them, as a snapshot **at that event**. Later events inherit it until you record something different. This is the whole model — see [Core Concepts](Core-Concepts).

---

## Step 7: Add a map (optional)

1. Go to **Maps**.
2. **Upload** a map image, or **link** one by URL.
3. Use **+ Location** in the floating toolbar and click the canvas to drop a marker.

No map image? **Generate locations with AI** builds a whole tree of places as pins on a blank map — see [Maps](Maps).
