# Factions

Factions are the organisations your characters belong to — kingdoms, guilds, cults, fellowships.

---

## A faction

Each faction has a **colour**, a description, an image, and tags. The colour is what identifies it everywhere else in the app.

---

## Members

Add characters as members with:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Their position — Leader, Spy, Recruit |
| **Start event** | When the membership begins; blank means always |
| **End event** | When it ends; blank means ongoing |
| **Notes** | Freeform |

Scoped memberships are used by the [Continuity Checker](Continuity-Checker) to catch a character acting as a member outside their window.

A character's own profile has a **Factions** tab listing their memberships with roles and event ranges.

---

## Faction relationships

Record the stance between two factions — allied, hostile, neutral, and so on — with optional notes.

---

## Territories

**Regions and location markers can name an owning faction.** Those assignments appear under **Territories** on the faction's detail panel, so a faction's page tells you both who is in it and what it holds.

On the map, a marker or region assigned to a faction picks up that faction's colour.

---

## Overlays

Turn on the **Factions overlay** in the [Relationships graph](Relationships) or the [Character Arc grid](Character-Arc) to colour characters by their **active membership at the selected event** — so a defection or a merger reads visually, across the whole book.

---

## Generating factions with AI

**Generate with AI** creates new factions and updates matching ones in place, **merging their members in rather than dropping them**.

Members reference characters **by name**: only names that already exist are linked, unknown names are ignored, and no characters are created. **Generate your cast first.**

See [Generating with AI](AI-Generation).

---

## Common problems

**A character still shows as a member after leaving.**
Set the membership's **End event**. Blank means ongoing.

**Faction colours aren't on the map.**
Confirm the marker or region has the faction set, and that the faction has a colour.
