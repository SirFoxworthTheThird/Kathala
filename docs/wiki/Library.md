# The Library

**Library** on the world selector lists story bibles built from published books and downloads them straight into your browser.

Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. A downloaded world is an ordinary world you own outright — edit it, export it, or delete it like any other.

---

## Downloading a book

1. Click **Library** on the world selector.
2. Each entry shows its cast, chapter and event counts, and the download size.
3. Click the entry to download the world.

**Some books carry their pictures, most link to them.** Map images and portraits are much larger than the structural data — one book is 587 KB of story against 15 MB of pictures — so where a book has them the card says **Embedded images** and the size on the **Download** button is the whole of what will be fetched. Everywhere else the card says **Pictures load from the web**, and *World settings → Pictures* will take a local copy afterwards if you want one.

### Covers

Books whose cover is a *linked* image show it on the card. That image is fetched from wherever it is hosted, so opening the Library makes a request to that site. A cover that cannot be loaded simply goes missing rather than showing a broken frame.

Books whose artwork lives inside the image bundle stay text-only here — opening the catalogue is not asking for a book, and drawing those covers would mean pulling tens of megabytes down to decorate a card you may scroll past.

## Downloaded worlds arrive in reading mode

Library worlds open in **[reading mode](Reading-Mode)**, at their first moment rather than fully revealed, so nothing is given away before you have read a word.

Set the chapter cursor to where you have read up to and PlotWeave hides everything the story has not introduced yet — characters, items, places, lore, subplot names, and knowledge facts — across the whole app. Move the cursor forward and they appear, in the order the book introduces them.

PlotWeave remembers your place in each book, and the world card on your shelf says *"Chapter 5 of 17"* with a progress bar.

Read the full behaviour on [Reading Mode](Reading-Mode).

---

## Where downloaded books live

As soon as one world is in reading mode, the world selector splits into two shelves:

- **Your worlds** — the ones you are writing. The *New World* tile lives here.
- **Reading** — downloaded books, below.

A shelf of downloaded books therefore never buries your own work. Turning reading mode off in a world's settings moves it back up to *Your worlds*, since at that point you are editing rather than reading it. With nothing in reading mode there is one list and no headings at all.

---

## Re-downloading

> **Downloading a world you already have replaces your copy** and discards any changes you made in it. PlotWeave asks first. Use it deliberately when you want the original back.

---

## What library worlds contain

**Most of them carry the book itself.** 34 of the 41 are complete public-domain novels, and each one's catalogue entry names the Project Gutenberg edition its text came from. On those, reading mode adds a **Read** screen — see [Reading Mode](Reading-Mode).

The other seven carry **no text**, and are structural references only: characters, chapters, events, places, relationships and lore. Those are the books still in copyright, plus *Journey to the West*. Every world carries the structural layer either way, and all of it — summaries, descriptions, status notes — is original editorial writing rather than the author's words.

They are unofficial and fan-made, and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the authors or publishers.
