# Motifs & Themes

**Motifs & Themes** works exactly like [Plot Threads](Plot-Threads), but for **symbolism rather than plot** — recurring images, symbols, and themes such as *mirrors*, *the colour red*, or *exile*.

---

## Setting motifs up

1. Define named motifs on the [Dashboard](Dashboard), each with a colour.
2. Tag the scenes that carry them **from the event card**.

---

## The cadence strip

The dashboard draws a **cadence strip per motif** across your chapters so you can see its rhythm at a glance, and flags where a motif loses momentum:

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **vanishes for N chapters** | A motif that drops out of the middle of the book |
| **fades out** | A motif introduced early and then never seen again |

---

## What it's for

Use it to check that a theme you care about is **woven through the whole story**, not just raised once and forgotten. A motif that appears in chapters 1–3 and never again is usually either a thread you dropped or a symbol that wanted to be a one-off; the strip tells you which conversation to have with yourself.

---

## Hidden while reading

Motif names give away where a theme lands, so [reading mode](Reading-Mode) holds one back until the first scene that takes it up, and hides the dashboard panel.
