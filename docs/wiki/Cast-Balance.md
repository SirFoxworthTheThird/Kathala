# Cast Balance

Found on the [Dashboard](Dashboard), **Cast Balance** answers *"who is actually carrying this book?"*

---

## What it shows

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Screen-time bar** | Word-weighted, measured from your scene prose — not a count of appearances |
| **Presence strip** | Which chapters each character appears in |
| **Flags** | Automatic warnings when someone important drops out for a long stretch, or goes quiet late in the story |

Because the bar is weighted by the words in the scenes a character is in, a character who appears briefly in many scenes reads differently from one who dominates a few long ones.

---

## What it catches

- A **protagonist who vanishes** for ten chapters.
- A **side character who has quietly taken over**.
- A character who **fades out** before the ending needs them.

---

## Prerequisites

Cast Balance measures **scene prose**, so it needs prose to measure. Write scenes in the [Manuscript](Manuscript) view (or [import a draft](Import-Manuscript)) and make sure events list their **involved characters**.

---

## Hidden while reading

Cast Balance measures the manuscript rather than the story, so [reading mode](Reading-Mode) hides it.
