# Knowledge

The Knowledge tracker manages **who knows what, and when they learn it** — the backbone of mysteries and dramatic irony.

---

## Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Fact** | A secret or key piece of information |
| **Reader learns at** | The event at which the *reader* is let in on it |
| **Reveal** | A record that a specific character learned the fact at a specific event |

---

## Recording a fact

1. Go to **Knowledge**.
2. Record a fact — a secret, a hidden identity, a death nobody has reported yet.
3. Mark when the **reader** learns it.
4. Log **reveals** to individual characters at specific events.

---

## Suggestions

**PlotWeave suggests facts from your story.** For example, from a character's death snapshot it will offer *"Gandalf the Grey is dead · Ch. 17"* as a fact worth tracking.

### Might also know

After one character learns a fact, **Might also know** looks for other characters who shared a later scene with a knower.

Accept a suggestion to add the likely reveal, or leave it untracked when the information was not actually shared. This is the fastest way to keep a secret's spread honest as the cast moves around.

---

## Generating facts with AI

**Generate with AI** adds facts in bulk. A fact's `origin`, `readerLearnsAt`, and each reveal reference **existing events by title** and **existing characters by name** — so add your timeline and cast first.

Anything that doesn't match is simply left unlinked; the fact is still created. A fact with a matching title is updated in place, with its reveals merged in, so re-running never duplicates.

See [Generating with AI](AI-Generation).

---

## Why it matters

Paired with the [Continuity Checker](Continuity-Checker), knowledge tracking catches **a character acting on information they shouldn't have yet** — the single hardest continuity error to spot by rereading.

---

## While reading

In [reading mode](Reading-Mode), a knowledge fact stays hidden until the point the reader is meant to learn it, and a fact's page shows what it is and who knows it without the controls that set the reveal.
