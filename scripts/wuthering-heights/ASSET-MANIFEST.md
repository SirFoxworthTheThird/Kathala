# Wuthering Heights generated-asset manifest

All 42 JPEG assets in `public/library/wuthering-heights/` are original illustrations generated for Kathala with OpenAI ImageGen. They are repository-hosted and are not photographs, film/television likenesses, scans, or reused map images. Each asset was generated separately and visually reviewed before inclusion.

## Shared art direction

- Period: Yorkshire, approximately 1771–1802.
- Tone: mature Gothic historical illustration; restrained, windswept, psychologically serious.
- Character and location art: nineteenth-century oil-painting and engraved-book-illustration sensibility, without text or modern objects.
- Exclusions in every prompt: photography, actors, cartoons, childish styling, modern clothing, labels, watermarks, and unrelated maps.

## Character portraits

The sixteen `art/character-*.jpg` files were separately prompted from the corresponding character definition: age, social role, temperament, and period clothing. Catherine and Cathy, Edgar and Linton, and Heathcliff and Hareton were explicitly differentiated so related characters remain recognisable without sharing an illustration.

## Location illustrations

The fifteen `art/location-*.jpg` files were separately prompted as eye-level views of the named place and its source-grounded physical character. These are atmospheric location illustrations, distinct from the four navigational maps.

## Item illustrations

The six `art/item-*.jpg` files were separately prompted as semantically exact still lifes: Catherine's marginal diary, Lockwood's storm lantern, Heathcliff's money and account papers, Cathy and Linton's letters, Linton's legal will, and Hareton's reading books.

## Cover

`art/world-cover.jpg` was prompted as a text-free vertical Gothic cover showing two adult figures separated by the moor, with the Heights prominent and the Grange distant. It is not used for any other entity.
